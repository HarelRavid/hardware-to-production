## Relative architecture estimate
Using the 11 top-level WBS sections only as a rough navigation metric:
- clearly architecture-complete canonical sections: 3, 4, 5 = 3/11
- Section 6 is underway
- Sections 0, 1, 2 and 9 already contain meaningful prior work but require reconciliation/audit before being counted as complete

Therefore it would be misleading to state that the whole project is only 27% complete: a large amount of foundation predates the canonical WBS. It would also be misleading to call it >50% complete overall because evidence population and verification remain much larger workstreams.

## Phase transition ahead
Once canonical architecture is closed, the project must deliberately switch from architecture-first work to evidence-first population:
- standards
- handbooks/books
- peer-reviewed papers
- manufacturer/industry guidance
- case studies
- quantitative process windows/capability evidence
- claim verification
- episode research packs

## Critical warning
Do not interpret Architecture COMPLETE as Podcast Ready.
Architecture tells us what must be known. Evidence population proves what we can responsibly say.
