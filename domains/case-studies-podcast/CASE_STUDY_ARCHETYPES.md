# 10.1–10.8 Case Study Archetypes & Cross-Domain Stories

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Case-study families
### Hardware launch failures
- late DFM changes
- immature suppliers
- tooling/capability surprises
- test bottlenecks
- reliability escapes
- configuration/change-control failures

### Successful industrialization patterns
- early production-intent builds
- strong supplier co-development
- risk-based test/inspection
- staged automation
- disciplined configuration/effectivity
- rapid defect learning loops

### Startup manufacturing debt
- prototype processes carried too long
- undocumented tribal knowledge
- underbuilt quality/traceability
- late tooling investment
- supplier single points of failure
- heroic engineering labor masking instability

### Industry lenses
Automotive, medical-device, aerospace and industrial-equipment cases should capture methods, constraints and context rather than treating an industry as a monolithic best-practice source.

## Cross-domain story template
1. Decision/problem
2. Product/lifecycle context
3. Constraints and assumptions
4. Options considered
5. Evidence available at the time
6. Decision/action
7. Process/supply/quality consequences
8. Outcome
9. Mechanism interpretation
10. What should have been known earlier?
11. Transferable lesson
12. Limits of transfer
13. Linked WBS domains/Decision Objects

## Bias controls
- survivorship bias
- hindsight bias
- outcome bias
- single-source corporate narrative
- cherry-picked metric
- confounding redesign/process change
- undocumented external market/supply effects

## Decision objects
### D-CASE-MECH-001 — What failure/success mechanism is actually evidenced?
### D-CASE-XFER-001 — Which lesson transfers to another hardware context and under what assumptions?
### D-CASE-SRC-001 — Is source diversity sufficient to avoid relying on a single retrospective narrative?

## Integrity rule
A famous company name does not increase the evidence maturity of a case.
