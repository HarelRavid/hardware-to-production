## Show-note source pack
Each episode should publish/retain a source pack that separates source classes and makes the engineering trail reviewable.

## Source-pack sections
- standards/regulations
- books/handbooks
- peer-reviewed papers
- manufacturer/industry guidance
- case-study/report sources
- quantitative datasets/tools
- glossary/definitions
- episode-specific caveats and unresolved conflicts

## Source metadata
- source ID
- title/organization/author
- publication/revision date
- source-quality rank
- normative/informative role
- linked claim(s)
- linked case(s)
- applicability/scope
- access/link metadata
- reviewed date

## Publication principle
Show notes are not merely a list of links. They should allow a technically motivated listener to reconstruct the evidence behind important episode claims and identify where the episode contains synthesis or inference.

6. Quantitative examples retain assumptions/units/sources.
7. Synthesis/inference is distinguishable from source fact.
8. Open questions remain visible rather than rhetorically closed.
9. Show-note sources reconstruct the evidence trail.
