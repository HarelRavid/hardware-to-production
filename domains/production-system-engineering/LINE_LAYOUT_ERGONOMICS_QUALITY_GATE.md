## Layout principles
- minimize unnecessary transport/motion
- preserve routing and material-status clarity
- protect bottleneck flow
- separate incompatible dirty/clean or hazardous processes where required
- design maintenance and changeover access
- place quality gates where evidence is needed
- preserve safe personnel/material interaction
- support future volume/product-mix change where justified

## Ergonomics objects
- reach
- working height
- posture
- force
- lift/carry
- repetition
- duration
- visibility
- tool reaction force
- fixture loading
- cognitive load
- error opportunity

## Engineering principle
Ergonomics is a production-capability variable as well as a safety/human-factor concern. A method that only achieves takt through unsustainable force, reach, repetition or cognitive load is not stable Standard Work.

## Integrity gates before Podcast Ready
1. EBOM and MBOM are distinct controlled objects.
2. Routing defines product-state transitions, not only work centers.
3. Tooling/fixtures are revision controlled and qualified where they affect conformity.
4. WI, Standard Work and operator qualification are distinct but linked.
5. PFMEA risks link to actual prevention/detection/control-plan actions.
6. Test/calibration systems are revision controlled and traceable.
7. Calibration is not substituted for MSA.
8. Material status and traceability survive internal logistics.
9. Layout and ergonomics are linked to flow, quality and sustainable takt.
10. GNR remains visible until verified.
