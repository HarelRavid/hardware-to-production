# 3.7 Polymer Forming — Case Study Backlog

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## 3.7.1 Purpose
Define case-study structures needed to convert process knowledge into engineering decisions.

## 3.7.2 Planned cases
1. Extrusion vs machined profile for a long constant-cross-section component.
2. Blow molding vs injection-molded multi-part container.
3. Thermoforming vs injection molding for a large thin shell/enclosure.
4. Rotational molding vs welded/fabricated tank or housing.
5. Compression molding vs injection molding for a filled/reinforced thermoset part.
6. Transfer molding for encapsulation/insert-rich geometry.
7. RIM vs conventional injection molding for a large low-pressure molded component.
8. Prototype made by CNC/3DP that falsely validated geometry but not production wall-thickness behavior.
9. Production failure caused by secondary trimming or leak testing becoming the true bottleneck.
10. Tooling released before material/process shrinkage behavior was sufficiently characterized.

## 3.7.3 Required case-study fields
- initial requirement
- initial process assumption
- actual constraints
- evidence used
- design changes
- tooling changes
- quality implications
- economics
- NPI lesson
- what could have been known earlier

## 3.7.4 Evidence requirement
Case studies remain GNR placeholders until tied to published cases, supplier evidence, or documented industrial examples.
