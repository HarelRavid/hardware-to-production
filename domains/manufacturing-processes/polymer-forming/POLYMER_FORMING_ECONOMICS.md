# 3.7 Polymer Forming — Economics & Scale-up

status: Researching

## 3.7.1 Cost structure
For each process, model:
- tooling/NRE
- material utilization
- cycle time / line speed
- labor
- secondary trimming/machining
- scrap/regrind
- changeover
- inspection
- utilities
- automation
- maintenance
- packaging/logistics where geometry creates shipping penalty

## 3.7.2 Scale-up questions
1. What changes between prototype tooling and production tooling?
2. Which dimensions are controlled by tooling versus process conditions?
3. What process window is required before volume ramp?
4. Which secondary operations become bottlenecks at scale?
5. Can material regrind be used without violating product/property requirements?

## 3.7.3 Process-specific economics
### Extrusion
Economics often depend strongly on continuous line utilization, die/changeover, downstream sizing/cooling/cutting and scrap during startup.

### Blow molding
Economics depend on machine cycle, mold cavities, parison/preform control, trimming, leak testing and resin mass.

### Thermoforming
Sheet cost and trim scrap can dominate material economics; nesting and trim reuse require material- and quality-specific validation.

### Rotational molding
Cycle time, mold count, heating/cooling and manual handling can dominate; tooling may be lower-cost than some high-pressure molding processes but throughput is typically lower.

### Compression/Transfer molding
Tooling, cure time, charge/preform preparation, flash/trim and press utilization are key.

### RIM
Meter-mix equipment, reactive chemistry control, mold utilization, cure/demold and post-cure/finishing can dominate.

## 3.7.4 Decision object
D-POLYFORM-ECON-001 — At what volume/maturity should the process/tooling strategy change?

Inputs: forecast, product maturity, tooling investment, expected changes, unit-cost target, line utilization, secondary operations, scrap, quality risk.

## 3.7.5 Evidence rule
No universal volume break-even thresholds are published without an explicit cost model and scoped supplier/process data.
