# 3.7 Polymer Forming — Standards & Source Map

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR, STD]

## 3.7.1 Purpose
Map primary standards and authoritative source families relevant to extrusion, blow molding, thermoforming, rotational molding, compression molding, transfer molding and reaction injection molding.

## 3.7.2 Current anchor standards
### Moulded-part tolerances
- ISO 20457:2018 — Plastics moulded parts — Tolerances and acceptance conditions. Confirmed in 2024 and currently applicable; ISO Edition 2 is under publication in 2026 and is expected to replace the 2018 edition. Scope includes injection, injection-compression, transfer, compression and rotational molding of non-porous molded parts.

### Compression molding test-specimen preparation
- ISO 293:2023 — compression molding of thermoplastic test specimens.
- ISO 295:2004 — compression molding of thermosetting test specimens.

### Thermoset molding shrinkage / flow
- ISO 2577:2007 — determination of molding shrinkage and post-shrinkage of thermosetting molding materials.
- ISO 7808:1992 — determination of transfer flow for thermosetting molding materials.

### Extrusion-relevant material behavior
- ISO 16790:2021 — drawing characteristics of thermoplastics in the molten state; relevant to interpretation of extensional-flow behavior of extrusion-capable materials.

### Blow-molded product-specific example
- ISO 13106:2014 — blow-molded polypropylene containers for liquid-food packaging. Product-specific, not a universal blow-molding design standard.

## 3.7.3 Standards governance
For every standard:
- preserve edition/year;
- record whether current, confirmed, under revision or superseded;
- separate process standards from product-specific standards;
- do not infer universal DFM rules from test-specimen preparation standards;
- connect each clause-derived claim to exact scope and applicability.

## 3.7.4 Evidence gaps
Need targeted standards/guides for:
- thermoforming dimensional capability and wall-thickness distribution;
- blow molding parison/preform programming and container qualification;
- rotational molding process control and product acceptance;
- RIM process control and reactive-system quality;
- extrusion die design, dimensional stability and line control.

## 3.7.5 Source hierarchy
1. ISO/ASTM/EN/industry standards
2. polymer-material suppliers and machinery OEM technical documentation
3. recognized plastics engineering handbooks
4. peer-reviewed polymer-processing literature
5. qualified industrial case studies
6. GNR synthesis pending verification
