# 3.7 Polymer Forming — Engineering Claim Registry

All claims are GNR unless external provenance is added after verification.

## H2P-CLM-PF-001 — Extrusion is governed by continuous-section logic
Statement: Polymer extrusion is most naturally suited to products whose geometry is generated continuously along a primary axis; deviations from this logic generally require downstream operations, secondary forming, machining or assembly.
Classification: Project Synthesis
Confidence: High
Provenance: [GNR, SYN]

## H2P-CLM-PF-002 — Prototype geometry can misrepresent extrusion behavior
Statement: A machined or additively manufactured prototype of an extrusion profile may validate geometry and assembly but does not reproduce extrusion-specific orientation, cooling history, residual stress or die-flow behavior.
Classification: Project Synthesis
Confidence: High
Provenance: [GNR, SYN]

## H2P-CLM-PF-003 — Blow molding requires wall-distribution thinking
Statement: In blow molding, nominal CAD wall thickness is not sufficient to describe the produced part because material stretching, parison/preform behavior and geometry can cause nonuniform wall-thickness distribution.
Classification: Project Synthesis
Confidence: High
Provenance: [GNR, SYN]

## H2P-CLM-PF-004 — Leak testing should be considered during blow-molded product/process design
Statement: For hollow blow-molded products whose function depends on containment, leak-test method, access, takt and reaction strategy should be considered before serial-production release.
Classification: Industrial Best Practice / Project Synthesis
Confidence: High
Provenance: [GNR, IND, SYN]

## H2P-CLM-PF-005 — Thermoforming transforms sheet, not bulk melt
Statement: Thermoforming DFM must account for the initial sheet state and local stretching, making wall-thickness distribution and trim strategy central design concerns.
Classification: Project Synthesis
Confidence: High
Provenance: [GNR, SYN]

## H2P-CLM-PF-006 — Rotational molding does not imply injection-like dimensional capability
Statement: Rotational molding can be attractive for large hollow parts and relatively low tooling pressure/cost, but functional interfaces may still require secondary machining or dedicated inspection when tighter geometry is needed.
Classification: Project Synthesis
Confidence: Medium-High
Provenance: [GNR, SYN]

## H2P-CLM-PF-007 — Reactive molding requires cure/process-state control
Statement: Compression, transfer and reaction-injection processes that depend on reaction or cure require control of material state and cure-related variables in addition to geometric mold control.
Classification: Project Synthesis
Confidence: High
Provenance: [GNR, SYN]

## H2P-CLM-PF-008 — Tooling economics must be evaluated with secondary operations
Statement: Polymer-forming process economics should include trimming, machining, welding, printing, leak testing and inspection rather than comparing primary mold/die cost and nominal cycle time alone.
Classification: Project Synthesis
Confidence: High
Provenance: [GNR, SYN]

## H2P-CLM-PF-009 — Prototype representativeness is process-specific
Statement: A prototype can be functionally representative without being manufacturing-process representative; the required fidelity should be defined before using prototype results to release production tooling.
Classification: Project Synthesis
Confidence: Very High
Provenance: [GNR, SYN]

## H2P-CLM-PF-010 — Quantitative DFM rules require scoped evidence
Statement: Draft, draw ratios, wall limits, tolerances and process windows should not be published as universal polymer-forming limits when they originate from a particular material, geometry, supplier or machine context.
Classification: Knowledge Governance / Project Synthesis
Confidence: Very High
Provenance: [GNR, SYN]
