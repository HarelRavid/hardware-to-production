## Engineering principle
A coating test result is evidence for the specific coating/substrate/preparation/application/cure/test system. Accelerated corrosion exposure is not automatically a direct service-life conversion.

## Questions
1. Which failure mode is the coating intended to prevent?
2. Which test actually demonstrates that function?
3. Are edges, holes, threads, recesses and contact points included in inspection coverage?
4. Is the coating system compatible with galvanic couples created by assembly?
5. Which defects are cosmetic versus functional/reliability critical?

## Integrity rule
Do not infer years of field life from salt-spray/chamber hours without application-specific validated correlation.
