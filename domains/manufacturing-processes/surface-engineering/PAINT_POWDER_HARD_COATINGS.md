## DFM questions
- Can the part tolerate cure/deposition temperature?
- Will coating build alter fit, threads or sealing surfaces?
- Can edges/recesses/internal channels receive acceptable coverage?
- Is masking manufacturable and repeatable?
- Does the coating need conductivity, insulation, wear or corrosion performance?
- Can damaged coating be repaired without compromising the substrate/process chain?

## Failure modes
- poor adhesion
- orange peel/texture defects
- runs/sags boundary
- pinholes/porosity
- thin edges
- excessive build
- cure defects
- contamination inclusions
- blistering
- cracking/chipping
- overspray/mask leakage
