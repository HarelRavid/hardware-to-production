## Rework principle
A finish may be removable while the component is not fully recoverable. Stripping and reprocessing can change dimensions, roughness, substrate condition, fatigue/corrosion behavior or qualification state.

## Cost architecture
Cost per good part may include:
- surface preparation
- masking/unmasking
- racking/fixturing
- chemicals/coating material
- bath/line time
- spray/application labor
- cure/bake energy and occupancy
- inspection/testing
- environmental controls/waste treatment
- rework/stripping
- scrap due to finish defects
- logistics to external finisher
- packaging/protection after finish

## Decision objects

### D-SURF-AUTO-001 — Manual, semi-automatic or automated finishing route?
### D-SURF-MAKEBUY-001 — In-house or outsource finishing?

## Production questions
- Is masking labor the dominant cost rather than coating application?
- Does outsourcing create handling/lead-time/corrosion/traceability risk?
- Is line automation justified by volume, geometry repeatability and chemistry/process-control burden?
- Does rework preserve the original engineering/qualification basis?

No universal allowable rework count is asserted.
