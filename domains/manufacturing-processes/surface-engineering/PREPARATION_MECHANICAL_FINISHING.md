## Engineering questions
- Is the objective cleanliness, roughness change, edge condition, stress state, appearance or adhesion promotion?
- Does mechanical finishing remove material that affects tolerance/GD&T?
- Can embedded abrasive or cross-contamination create downstream risk?
- Should finishing occur before or after heat treatment, joining or machining?
- Is the generated surface appropriate for the next coating/bonding process?

## Failure modes
- residual contamination
- over-etch/over-blast
- embedded media
- excessive material removal
- dimensional change
- edge rounding beyond requirement
- nonuniform texture
- media carryover
- recontamination before coating
