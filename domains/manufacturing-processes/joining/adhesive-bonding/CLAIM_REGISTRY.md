# Adhesive Bonding — Engineering Claim Registry

All AI-originated Claims retain `GNR` provenance per ADR-003 and OS v1.0.

## H2P-CLM-ADH-001 — Surface preparation is part of the bonding process

**Statement:** Structural adhesive joint performance depends materially on controlled adherend surface preparation, so preparation should be specified and managed as a manufacturing process input rather than treated as incidental cleaning.

- Classification: Standards-Based Guidance / Project Synthesis
- Confidence: Very High
- Evidence maturity: L1
- Provenance: [GNR, STD, SYN]
- Applicable context: structural adhesive bonding of commonly encountered metals and plastics
- Limitations: exact preparation depends on substrate, adhesive and service environment
- Evidence: ISO 17212:2012

## H2P-CLM-ADH-002 — Coupon lap-shear strength is not a universal joint allowable

**Statement:** Apparent shear strength measured on a standardized single-lap specimen should not be used directly as a universal design allowable for structurally different adhesive joints without analysis of joint geometry and adhesive behavior.

- Classification: Normative Test-Method Context / Project Synthesis
- Confidence: Very High
- Evidence maturity: L1
- Provenance: [GNR, STD, SYN]
- Applicable context: bonded metal joint design using lap-shear test data
- Evidence: ASTM D1002
- Supports Decisions: adhesive test-plan selection; joint allowable development

## H2P-CLM-ADH-003 — Bonding quality requires fabrication records, not only final inspection

**Statement:** Risk control for adhesively bonded structures depends on controlled fabrication and reporting records because many process variables affecting bond quality cannot be reconstructed reliably from final visual inspection alone.

- Classification: Standards-Based Guidance / Project Synthesis
- Confidence: High
- Evidence maturity: L1
- Provenance: [GNR, STD, SYN]
- Applicable context: structural adhesive fabrication
- Evidence: ISO 21368:2022
- Supports Decisions: NPI traceability; work-instruction content; process control

## H2P-CLM-ADH-004 — Initial bond strength is insufficient to establish service durability

**Statement:** Initial mechanical strength alone is insufficient to establish long-term suitability of a bonded joint when environmental exposure can degrade the adhesive/adherend interface.

- Classification: Standards-Based Guidance / Project Synthesis
- Confidence: High
- Evidence maturity: L1
- Provenance: [GNR, STD, SYN]
- Applicable context: structural adhesive joints exposed to service environments
- Evidence: ISO 10354:1992; ISO adhesive durability framework
- Supports Decisions: qualification planning; environmental test planning

## H2P-CLM-ADH-005 — Prototype manual success does not establish serial-process capability

**Statement:** A bonded prototype assembled successfully by a skilled engineer does not by itself demonstrate that the same joint can be reproduced reliably at serial-production scale unless surface preparation, dispense/mix, bondline, cure, fixturing and inspection controls are formalized.

- Classification: Project Synthesis
- Confidence: High
- Evidence maturity: L1
- Provenance: [GNR, STD, SYN]
- Applicable context: prototype-to-production transition for adhesive bonding
- Evidence: ISO 21368:2022 quality/fabrication principles
- Supports Decisions: NPI readiness; automation strategy; process validation
