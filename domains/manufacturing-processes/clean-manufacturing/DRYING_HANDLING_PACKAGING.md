## Engineering principle
The clean state has a lifecycle. Cleaning without preservation can move the contamination problem downstream rather than solve it.

### D-CLEAN-DRY-001 — Drying strategy

## Failure modes
- recontamination
- moisture retention
- corrosion after cleaning
- packaging fibers/particles
- fingerprints/handling films
- wrong caps/plugs
- clean/dirty container mixing
- unprotected internal ports

Quantitative environmental/hold-time limits remain application-specific evidence objects.
