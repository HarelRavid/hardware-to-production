# 3.13.2 Cleaning Processes & Compatibility

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Cleaning mechanism objects
- dissolution
- emulsification
- saponification boundary
- surfactant action
- chelation boundary
- mechanical agitation
- spray impingement
- ultrasonic cavitation
- flushing/displacement
- chemical etching/oxide removal boundary

## Process variables
- chemistry
- concentration
- pH
- temperature
- exposure time
- agitation/flow
- ultrasonic power/frequency boundary
- bath loading
- filtration
- rinse sequence
- water purity
- drying condition

## Compatibility risks
- corrosion/etching
- stress-corrosion/environmental cracking boundary
- swelling/attack of polymers/elastomers
- adhesive/coating degradation
- residue deposition
- galvanic effects in mixed assemblies
- trapped chemistry in blind holes/crevices
- hydrogen-embrittlement boundary for susceptible materials/processes
- dimensional/surface change

## DFM questions
1. Can all wetted cavities be drained and dried?
2. Are blind holes/crevices creating chemical traps?
3. Is cleaning performed before or after assembly/coating/joining?
4. Can dissimilar materials in an assembly tolerate the same chemistry?
5. Does the cleaning route alter a functional surface condition?
6. Is the cleaner itself becoming the next contaminant?
