## Manufacturing principle
A cure recipe is not treated as a simple oven temperature. Part thickness, tool thermal mass, resin kinetics, exotherm, pressure/vacuum and thermocouple placement can influence the actual material history.

## Production controls
- material lot and storage history
- freezer removal/out-time
- ply kit traceability
- layup traveler
- bag integrity
- thermocouple locations
- autoclave/oven recipe
- pressure/vacuum history
- cure data record
- post-cure inspection

## Failure modes

- ply misorientation
- bridging
- resin bleed variation
