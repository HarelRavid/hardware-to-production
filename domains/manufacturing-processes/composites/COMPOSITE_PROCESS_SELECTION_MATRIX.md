## Master decision logic
### D-COMP-MASTER-001 — Which composite manufacturing route should be used?
Inputs:
1. structural load case / performance requirement
2. laminate architecture
3. fiber and matrix system
4. geometry and size
5. cosmetic/surface requirements
6. tolerance/dimensional requirements
7. annual volume and takt
8. tooling/CAPEX
9. available equipment
10. labor/skill availability
11. inspection/NDT
12. qualification/regulatory burden
13. repairability
14. supplier capability
15. lifecycle cost

Output is a ranked candidate set with trade-offs, evidence, confidence, assumptions and open questions — not an automatic universal answer.

## Key comparison questions
- When is prepreg/autoclave actually justified versus infusion/RTM/OOA?
- When does manual layup cease to be the economically or technically robust route?
- When does a constant-section design justify pultrusion?
- When does hollow axisymmetric geometry justify filament winding?
- When does matched-tool closed molding justify its tooling investment?
- When does automation solve a repeatability bottleneck versus merely automate an unstable process?

## Cross-family alternatives
Composite process selection must also compare against:
- machined metal
- sheet metal/fabrication
- casting
- injection/compression molded polymer
- extrusion/pultrusion
- additive manufacturing

The question is not only "which composite process?" but sometimes "should this be composite at all?"
