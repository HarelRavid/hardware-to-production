## DFM questions
- Can the structure be cured as one piece or should it be assembled?
- How are inserts and hard points introduced without creating local manufacturing/inspection problems?
- Can drilling/trimming be referenced to stable post-cure datums?
- How will core edges and penetrations be sealed/protected?
- Does the joint require inspectability after assembly?

## Failure modes
- skin/core disbond
- core crush
- insert pull-out
- adhesive voids
- kissing/weak bond risk
- drilling delamination
- fiber breakout
- water/moisture ingress paths
- dimensional mismatch between cured subassemblies
