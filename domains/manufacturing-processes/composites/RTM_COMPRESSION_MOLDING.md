## DFM questions
- Can the reinforcement/preform be placed repeatably?
- Can resin reach and impregnate the complete reinforcement architecture?
- Where should gates and vents be located?
- Can the mold withstand pressure and thermal cycling while maintaining dimensional control?
- Does serial volume justify matched tooling and automation?
- Can trimming, inserts and post-mold operations be reduced?

## Failure modes
