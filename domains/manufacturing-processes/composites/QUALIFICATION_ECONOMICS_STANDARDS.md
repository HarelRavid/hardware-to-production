# 3.8.9 Qualification, Economics & Standards Map

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Qualification architecture
- material qualification
- process qualification
- tooling qualification
- operator qualification
- equipment qualification
- first article / first-off validation
- destructive coupon testing
- witness panels/coupons
- environmental conditioning
- NDT qualification
- change control and requalification

## Production economics
Cost model includes:
- reinforcement and resin/prepreg cost
- material waste and nesting
- consumables
- tooling and amortization
- layup labor
- cure equipment time
- machine/autoclave occupancy
- inspection/NDT
- trim/machining
- scrap/rework
- automation
- freezer/storage/logistics where applicable
- qualification/NRE

Cost per good part and capacity risk are preferred over simple material or tooling cost.

## Standards/source families to populate

- FAA/EASA/NASA guidance where applicable

## Evidence rules
- test method does not automatically define design allowable
- material coupon property does not automatically equal structural part property
- aerospace specification does not automatically apply to general industrial product
- customer-specific acceptance limits remain scoped to that context

## Decisions

### D-COMP-ECO-001 — Which process minimizes lifecycle cost for required performance/volume?
### D-COMP-CHANGE-001 — Does this material/process/tool change require requalification?
