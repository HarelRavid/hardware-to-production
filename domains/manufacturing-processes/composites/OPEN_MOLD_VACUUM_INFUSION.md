- Can the geometry be bagged without bridging?
- Can resin flow reach all regions without unacceptable race tracking or dry zones?
- Are ply drops, corners and thickness transitions manufacturable?
- Can consumables be installed and removed repeatably?
- What process evidence proves vacuum integrity before resin/cure?
