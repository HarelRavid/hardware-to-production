# 3.6.6 Grinding & Precision Finishing

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Scope
- Surface grinding
- Cylindrical grinding
- Centerless grinding
- Internal grinding
- Honing
- Lapping
- Superfinishing

## Engineering meaning
Grinding and precision finishing are often secondary processes used where turning/milling alone cannot robustly achieve required geometry, surface condition, hardness-compatible stock removal or functional interface performance.

## DFM questions
- Is the requirement truly a grinding requirement or can turning/milling meet function?
- Does heat treatment occur before or after finish machining?
- What stock allowance must remain for finishing?
- Are datums and locating surfaces available after heat treatment?
- Is surface texture, geometry, size, or surface integrity the actual requirement?

## Key process concepts
- abrasive grain / bond / wheel structure
- wheel speed and work speed
- dressing and truing
- grinding ratio / wheel wear
- grinding burn
- residual stress
- thermal damage
- spark-out
- stock allowance
- honing crosshatch
- lapping flatness

## Failure modes
- grinding burn
- cracking
- chatter
- glazing/loading
- taper/out-of-round
- excessive roughness
- dimensional drift
- embedded abrasive/contamination

## Decision links
- hard turning vs grinding
- reaming vs honing
- milling vs surface grinding
- finish specification vs process selection
