# 3.6.4 Turning

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Definition
Turning removes material from a rotating workpiece using one or more cutting tools. Production turning includes external/internal turning, facing, grooving, threading, parting and combined live-tool operations on turning centers.

## Engineering meaning
Turning is especially efficient for rotational geometry, but production capability depends on workholding, stock condition, overhang/slenderness, tool access, thermal stability, chip control and measurement strategy.

## DFM questions
- Can the part remain rotationally symmetric enough to avoid a secondary milling setup?
- Which diameters/faces should share a setup to preserve coaxiality/runout relationships?
- Does a deep bore require drilling, boring, reaming, honing or grinding?
- Can shoulders/grooves/threads be reached with robust standard tooling?
- Is bar-fed production feasible, and what stock diameter/material form minimizes waste?

## Production risks
- chatter and deflection
- dimensional drift from tool wear/thermal effects
- poor chip control
- distortion from clamping
- runout from setup transfer
- burrs at cross holes/part-off

## Decision links
- Turn vs mill
- Conventional lathe vs Swiss-type
- Bore vs ream vs hone vs grind
- Bar stock vs near-net blank
