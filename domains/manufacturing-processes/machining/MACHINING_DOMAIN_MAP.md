# 3.6 Machining & Material Removal

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## 3.6.1 Domain Purpose
This domain covers material-removal processes as production technologies, with emphasis on DFM, process capability, surface integrity, cost, automation, quality, and transition from prototype machining to repeatable serial production.

## 3.6.2 Process Taxonomy
### Conventional cutting
- Turning
- Milling
- Drilling
- Reaming
- Boring
- Broaching
- Sawing

### Abrasive processes
- Surface grinding
- Cylindrical grinding
- Centerless grinding
- Honing
- Lapping
- Superfinishing

### Non-traditional removal
- Wire EDM
- Sinker EDM
- Laser material removal
- Abrasive waterjet
- Electrochemical machining

## 3.6.4 Questions this domain must answer
1. When should a part remain machined in serial production instead of moving to a near-net-shape process?
2. Which features are expensive because of tool access, setup count, reach, rigidity or inspection rather than nominal geometry alone?
3. How should tolerance and surface requirements be allocated to functional features?
4. How do material, tool, machine, workholding and toolpath interact to determine capability?
5. When is grinding/reaming/boring/EDM justified as a secondary process?
6. How should a prototype CNC process change when it becomes a production process?
7. Which process variables and signatures should be controlled or monitored?
8. How should machine capability be separated from part/process capability?
