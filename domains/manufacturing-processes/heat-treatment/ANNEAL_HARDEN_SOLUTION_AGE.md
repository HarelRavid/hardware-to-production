# 3.14.2 Annealing, Hardening, Tempering, Solution Treatment & Aging

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Process objects
### Annealing / stress relief
- recovery/recrystallization boundary
- residual-stress reduction
- softening/machinability
- dimensional stabilization
- oxidation/decarburization risk

### Hardening / tempering
- austenitizing boundary
- quench severity
- hardenability
- section response
- tempering
- hardness/toughness balance
- retained-phase boundary

### Solution treatment / aging
- solutionizing
- dissolution of phases
- quench
- natural/artificial aging boundary
- precipitation sequence
- overaging boundary

## DFM / process questions
1. Is heat treatment performed before rough machining, between rough and finish machining, or after final machining?
2. How much machining allowance is needed for thermal distortion/scale where applicable?
3. Does section thickness create property gradients?
4. Can the required cooling/quench occur without unacceptable cracking or distortion?
5. Do downstream coating, welding or service temperatures alter the heat-treated condition?

## Knowledge integrity
Named heat-treatment conditions are alloy/system-specific. Generic labels such as “T6”, “quenched and tempered” or “stress relieved” must retain the governing material specification and actual process scope.
