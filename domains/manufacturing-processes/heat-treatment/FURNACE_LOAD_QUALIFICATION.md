# 3.14.6 Furnace, Load & Process Qualification

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Qualification objects
- furnace class/capability boundary
- working zone
- temperature uniformity survey (TUS) boundary
- system accuracy test (SAT) boundary
- instrumentation type/calibration
- control/recording sensors
- load thermocouples
- production load pattern
- minimum/maximum load
- fixture/basket configuration
- atmosphere/vacuum capability
- quench-system capability
- maintenance/change control

## Production-load objects
- part count
- total mass
- section distribution
- stacking
- spacing
- orientation
- fixture thermal mass
- shielding
- gas circulation/access
- thermocouple placement

## Engineering principle
Qualification of an empty furnace or generic working zone does not by itself prove that every production load configuration creates the required part thermal history.

## Decision objects
### D-HT-LOAD-001 — Define qualified production-load envelope
### D-HT-TUS-001 — What furnace uniformity/instrumentation evidence is required by the governing specification/application?
### D-HT-PPQ-001 — Is an instrumented production-part/load qualification required?
### D-HT-CHANGE-001 — Which changes trigger requalification?

## Change examples to evaluate
- furnace repair/control-system change
- fixture/basket change
- load mass/count change
- part geometry/section change
- atmosphere/gas-flow change
- quench medium/agitation change
- recipe revision
- sensor/instrumentation change

Quantitative qualification intervals/tolerances require the applicable standard/specification and revision.
