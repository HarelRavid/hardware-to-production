## Process chain
Powder characteristics -> forming/green density -> debinding -> sintering kinetics -> densification/grain growth -> porosity/microstructure -> functional properties.

## Engineering questions
1. Which final properties are controlled by density versus microstructure versus chemistry?
2. How much green-density variation can the downstream sintering process tolerate?
3. Can increased densification create undesirable grain growth or distortion?
4. Which defects originate in powder preparation/forming but only become visible after sintering?
5. Which measurements belong in-process versus final inspection?

## Decision objects

Inputs: mechanical, electrical, thermal, permeability, catalytic/electrochemical or other functional requirement; material system; forming route; sintering route; machining and cost.

## Knowledge integrity
No universal density-property relationship is asserted. Relationships must be material-, process- and test-condition scoped.
