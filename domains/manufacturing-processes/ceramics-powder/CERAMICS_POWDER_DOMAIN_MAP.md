# 3.10 Ceramics & Powder-Based Manufacturing — Domain Map

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## 3.10.1 Scope
Manufacturing engineering for technical ceramics and powder-based parts from powder preparation through forming, debinding, sintering, finishing, inspection and scale-up.

## 3.10.2 Material families
- Oxide ceramics: alumina, zirconia, magnesia and others
- Non-oxide ceramics: silicon carbide, silicon nitride, borides/carbides/nitrides
- Glass-ceramics and specialty ceramics
- Metal powders / powder metallurgy boundary
- Composite powders / cermets

## 3.10.3 Process families
- Powder synthesis and conditioning
- Milling / mixing / deagglomeration
- Binder/plasticizer addition
- Spray drying / granulation
- Uniaxial pressing
- Cold isostatic pressing (CIP)
- Hot pressing / hot isostatic pressing (HIP)
- Slip casting
- Tape casting
- Ceramic injection molding (CIM)
- Extrusion
- Additive powder-based routes (cross-link only; detailed AM elsewhere)
- Debinding
- Sintering
- Reaction sintering / pressure-assisted sintering
- Green machining
- Hard machining / grinding / lapping

## 3.10.5 Core engineering questions
- Which forming route best matches geometry, material and production volume?
- How do powder size/shape/distribution and agglomeration affect forming and sintering?
- How should binder/debinding be designed to avoid cracking and residual defects?
- How can shrinkage and warpage be predicted and compensated?
- When does furnace loading or setter design dominate dimensional variation?
- Which defects are created during forming versus debinding versus sintering?
- Which dimensions should be formed near-net and which finished after sintering?
- How should lab-scale thermal cycles change when production mass and load increase?

## 3.10.6 Master decision object
### D-CER-MASTER-001 — Which powder/ceramic manufacturing route should be used?
Inputs: material, particle system, geometry, feature scale, wall thickness, tolerance, density/porosity target, surface requirement, annual volume, tooling budget, thermal cycle, atmosphere, inspection and supplier capability.

## 3.10.7 Knowledge integrity
No universal shrinkage percentage, debinding profile, sintering temperature, density target or furnace ramp is asserted without material/process/test scope and evidence.
