# 3.10.6 Green/Hard Machining, Inspection & Metrology

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Machining stages
- powder/preform trimming
- green machining
- bisque/pre-sinter machining where applicable
- hard machining after sintering
- grinding
- lapping/polishing
- laser/EDM boundary cases where material permits

## Engineering trade-off
Earlier machining may reduce tool difficulty/cost but must account for subsequent shrinkage and distortion. Final hard machining may improve final dimensional control but can be slower, costlier and capable of introducing surface/subsurface damage.

## Inspection objects
- incoming powder/particle characterization
- green dimensions
- green density/mass
- binder content
- debinding mass loss
- sintered dimensions
- density/porosity
- hardness
- surface roughness
- flatness/warpage
- microscopy/metallography
- phase/chemistry characterization
- crack detection/NDT
- functional testing

## Metrology principle
Measurement stage must be tied to the process decision it controls. A final measurement that only discovers upstream variation after a long furnace cycle may have poor manufacturing leverage.
