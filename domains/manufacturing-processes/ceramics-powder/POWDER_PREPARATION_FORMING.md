## DFM questions
1. Can geometry be formed without creating density gradients or weak green regions?
2. Does the powder require granulation for flow/fill consistency?
3. Is the slurry rheology compatible with tape/slip casting?
4. Should a feature be machined in green state instead of after sintering?
5. Can forming pressure or flow create anisotropy that matters after sintering?
