# 3.10.7 Powder Metallurgy, MIM & Process Boundaries

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Scope
Map shared powder-processing physics while preserving differences between technical ceramics, conventional metal powder metallurgy and metal injection molding (MIM).

## Conventional PM objects
- metal powder
- blending/lubrication
- die compaction
- green compact
- sintering
- sizing/coining
- infiltration/impregnation
- secondary heat treatment/machining

## MIM objects
- metal powder + binder feedstock
- injection molding
- debinding
- sintering
- shrinkage
- support/fixture strategy
- secondary finishing

## Shared objects
- particle size/shape
- packing
- binder/lubricant
- green strength
- debinding
- densification
- shrinkage
- atmosphere
- contamination
- furnace loading
- distortion

## Boundary principle
Similar process vocabulary does not imply interchangeable design rules. Ceramic injection molding, MIM and polymer injection molding differ in feedstock behavior, debinding, sintering, shrinkage and final property formation.

## Decision objects
### D-CPM-FAMILY-001 — Ceramic route, PM, MIM, casting, machining or additive?
Inputs: material, geometry, size, density/property requirement, tolerance, annual volume, tooling, secondary operations, inspection, supplier capability and total cost.

### D-MIM-001 — Is MIM justified versus machining or conventional PM?
No universal volume/size threshold is asserted.

## Cross-links
MIM <-> injection molding
PM/MIM <-> sintering
PM/MIM <-> machining
Powder processing <-> additive manufacturing
Debinding <-> thermal processing

All comparative rules remain GNR until evidence-linked.
