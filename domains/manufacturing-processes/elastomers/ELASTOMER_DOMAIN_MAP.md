# 3.9 Rubber & Elastomer Manufacturing — Domain Map

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## 3.9.1 Scope
Manufacturing engineering of rubber, silicone and elastomer components from development through serial production, including molding, extrusion, cure/vulcanization, seals, bonded assemblies, tooling, inspection, aging and automation.

## 3.9.2 Process families
- Compression molding
- Transfer molding
- Rubber injection molding
- Liquid silicone rubber (LSR) injection molding
- High-consistency rubber (HCR) molding
- Elastomer extrusion
- Continuous vulcanization
- Calendering
- Dipping/coating where relevant
- Overmolding / insert molding
- Rubber-to-metal bonding
- Post-cure operations

## 3.9.5 Core engineering questions
- Which elastomer process fits geometry, material, volume and quality requirements?
- How should cure and post-cure be controlled?
- How do shrinkage, flash and compression set affect DFM?
- How should seal geometry be validated for manufacture and life?
- When should LSR be preferred over HCR or conventional rubber molding?
- Which material/environment combinations require dedicated compatibility evidence?
- How should prototype seals/components represent production material and cure state?

## 3.9.6 Master decision object
### D-ELAST-MASTER-001 — Which elastomer manufacturing route should be used?
Inputs: polymer/compound, geometry, hardness, dimensions, sealing/function, environment, lifetime, volume, takt, tooling, cure constraints, automation, inspection, bonding, supplier capability.

## 3.9.7 Knowledge integrity
No universal shrinkage, cure, compression-set or compatibility values are asserted without material/compound/process context and evidence.
