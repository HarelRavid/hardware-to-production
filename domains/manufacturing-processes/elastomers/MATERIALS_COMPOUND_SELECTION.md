# 3.9.5 Elastomer Materials & Compound Selection

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Engineering principle
A generic statement such as “EPDM is compatible with X” is insufficient for a production decision without compound, temperature, concentration, exposure mode, duration and functional requirement.

## Decision objects
### D-ELAST-MAT-001 — Select elastomer compound
Inputs: fluid/gas, concentration, temperature, pressure, mechanical loading, motion, aging, permeability, cleanliness, regulatory requirements, manufacturing process and supplier capability.

### D-ELAST-HARD-001 — Select hardness range
Inputs: sealing deformation, assembly force, extrusion risk, wear, tolerance stack, temperature and compound capability.

## Knowledge conflicts to preserve
- compatibility charts from different suppliers may disagree because compound formulations and test conditions differ;
- hardness alone does not uniquely define mechanical response;
- short immersion tests may not represent long-life seal performance.

Quantitative recommendations remain evidence-scoped.
