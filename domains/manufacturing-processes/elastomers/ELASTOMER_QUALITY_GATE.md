## Required integrity checks before Podcast Ready
1. No generic compatibility claim without service scope.
2. No universal shrinkage or cure value.
3. Material family and compound are distinguished.
4. Cure/post-cure history is represented where performance depends on it.
5. Seal geometry is linked to application requirements and tolerance stack.
6. Prototype and serial-production processes are distinguished.
7. Failure modes link to process, aging and inspection/detection.
8. Standards carry revision and applicability.
9. Decision objects expose assumptions/open questions.
10. GNR content is visibly flagged until verified.
