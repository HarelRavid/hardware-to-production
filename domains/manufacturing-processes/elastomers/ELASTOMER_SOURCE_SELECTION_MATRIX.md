# 3.9.7 Elastomer Standards, Evidence & Process Selection

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Standards/evidence families to populate
### Material/property test methods
- hardness
- tensile/elongation
- tear
- compression set
- accelerated aging
- liquid immersion/swelling
- ozone/weathering
- low-temperature behavior

### Product/application standards
- O-rings and seal dimensions/tolerances
- aerospace/automotive/food/pharma application requirements where applicable
- hose/profile requirements
- rubber-to-metal bonded products

### Quality/process evidence
- compound certificate/lot data
- cure rheometry where applicable
- mold/process parameter records
- post-cure record
- dimensional/visual inspection
- functional leak/pressure testing when product requires it

Primary-source families to research: ISO, ASTM, SAE and application-specific standards; supplier data is retained as compound-specific evidence rather than universal truth.

## Master process-selection object
### D-ELAST-MASTER-001 — How should this elastomer component be produced?
Inputs:
- compound/material family
- thermoset vs LSR/TPE boundary
- geometry and section thickness
- tolerance
- surface/flash requirement
- insert/overmold requirement
- annual volume and takt
- tooling budget
- cure/post-cure requirement
- inspection
- environment/life
- supplier capability

Candidate routes:
- compression molding
- transfer molding
- injection molding
- LSR injection molding
- extrusion + splice/join
- die-cut/converted sheet
- standard purchased seal
- alternate polymer/process where appropriate

## Selection principle
The preferred route minimizes total lifecycle/manufacturing risk and cost while meeting functional requirements; it is not automatically the process with the shortest nominal molding cycle.
