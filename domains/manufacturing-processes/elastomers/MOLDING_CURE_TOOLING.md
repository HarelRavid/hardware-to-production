## DFM questions
- Is geometry compatible with demolding and flash removal?
- Are thick/thin transitions likely to complicate cure uniformity?
- Where should parting line and venting be placed relative to functional surfaces?
- Does the product require post-cure and how does that affect takt and traceability?
- Can inserts be located and retained repeatably during molding?

- surface defects
- insert movement
- dimensional drift
