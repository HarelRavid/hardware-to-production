# 1.3 NPI & Industrialization Ownership

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Scope
Define responsibility for converting a product design into a controlled, scalable manufacturing system while preventing gaps between engineering, operations, quality and supply chain.

## Core ownership objects
- product engineering
- manufacturing/process engineering
- NPI/industrialization lead
- quality
- supply chain/procurement
- supplier quality
- test engineering
- operations/production
- maintenance/automation
- regulatory/compliance boundary

## Ownership principle
Cross-functional participation is not the same as clear accountability. Every readiness deliverable, risk and gate should have an accountable owner and explicit supporting roles.

## Typical NPI deliverables
- production-intent design baseline
- MBOM/routing
- DFM/DFA closure
- tooling/equipment
- process specifications/recipes
- WI/standard work
- PFMEA/control plan
- inspection/test/DFT
- supplier readiness
- training/qualification
- pilot/PVT evidence
- yield/capacity plan
- change/effectivity control
- ramp plan and exit criteria

## Decision objects
### D-PDR-OWN-001 — Who is accountable for each industrialization deliverable?
### D-PDR-HAND-001 — When does ownership transition from development to operations?
### D-PDR-RISK-001 — Which readiness risks require cross-functional gate review?

## Handoff principle
Operational ownership should transfer with evidence, controls and unresolved-risk visibility — not merely with a calendar launch date.
