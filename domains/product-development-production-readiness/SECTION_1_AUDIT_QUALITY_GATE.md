## Key principles
1. Lifecycle labels are organization-specific; objective/evidence matters more than the acronym.
2. Prototype success does not prove production readiness.
3. Product readiness and manufacturing readiness are distinct but coupled.
4. Production intent is multidimensional rather than binary.
5. Readiness should not be collapsed into one average score.
6. Every gate requires objective evidence, acceptance criteria and ownership.
7. Temporary deviations and non-production-intent conditions limit conclusions.
8. Configuration/effectivity must be preserved in validation evidence.
9. Handoff to operations transfers with controls and unresolved-risk visibility.
10. GNR remains visible until verified.
