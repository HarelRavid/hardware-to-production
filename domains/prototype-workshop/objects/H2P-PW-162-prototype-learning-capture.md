# Prototype Learning Capture
## Definition
Prototype Learning Capture is the deliberate recording of what was learned from a prototype iteration, why the conclusion is justified, and what design/process decision follows from it.
## Minimum Learning Record
- question/hypothesis
- prototype/build ID
- configuration
- test or observation
- evidence/data link
- conclusion
- confidence/limitations
- decision or change triggered
- reusable lesson/tag
## Why It Matters
Without structured capture, prototype knowledge remains tacit and is repeatedly rediscovered. NASA's Lessons Learned system formalizes reviewed lessons with the driving event and recommendations so experience can influence future projects.
