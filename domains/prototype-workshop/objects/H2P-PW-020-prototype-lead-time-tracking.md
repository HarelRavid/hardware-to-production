# Prototype Lead-time Tracking
## Definition
Prototype Lead-time Tracking measures the elapsed time between a build trigger and the point at which the prototype is ready to produce useful engineering feedback.
## Why It Matters
Prototype lead time is not merely a scheduling metric. Research on new product development links prototyping lead time and organizational capability to overall development cycle time.
## Useful Measures
- CAD release/request to fabrication start
- fabrication start to part completion
- part completion to assembled prototype
- assembled prototype to test-ready state
- request-to-feedback lead time
- external vs internal lead time
- queue time vs touch time

## Evidence
Solarte Bolaños & Barbalho (2021) found prototype manufacturing lead-times useful in predicting NPD cycle time for complex mechatronic products. Clark & Fujimoto model product development as problem-solving cycles and show organizational/process capability affects engineering lead time.
