# Prototype Cost Tracking
## Definition
Prototype Cost Tracking records the true cost of prototype iterations, including purchased parts, internal machine time, external services, tooling, rework, scrap and engineering effort where useful.
## Why It Matters
Prototype decisions are often distorted by comparing supplier invoice price with internal material cost only. The relevant decision is usually the total cost of learning delivered by each route.
## Suggested Measures
- material and purchased-part cost
- external service cost
- tooling/fixture cost
- machine hours
- engineering/technician hours
- rework and scrap
- cost per useful iteration
- cost per validated learning objective
