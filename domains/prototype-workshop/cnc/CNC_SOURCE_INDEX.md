## Source Use Rules
1. Provider-specific numeric design values remain labeled provider-specific.
2. Supplier design guides may support manufacturability heuristics but do not override standards or validated process capability.
3. ASME/ISO documents are used for formal technical-definition context; exact edition/scope must be checked before normative claims.
4. NIST sources are preferred anchors for claims about machining as rapid prototyping and measurement/process behavior.
5. Manufacturer tooling literature is used to define tool families and application variables, not as an independent universal performance guarantee.
