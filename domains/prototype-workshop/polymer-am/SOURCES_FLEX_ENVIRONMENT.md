## Formlabs — Tough Resin V5
URL: https://formlabs.com/support/Using-Tough-Resin-V5/
Type: Manufacturer technical guidance
Use: Snap fits, living hinges, cyclic-loading suitability, high-temperature and constant-load limitations
Status: Reviewed 2026-08-07

Key contribution: current guidance positions Tough Resin V5 for high-stress functional prototypes, snap-fit joints, living hinges and cyclic loading, while explicitly excluding high-temperature applications and constant loading. Treat as formulation-specific industrial evidence.

## Yoshida & Wada — Mechanics of a snap-fit
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13566
Type: Academic mechanics paper/preprint
Use: Snap-fit mechanics: geometry, elasticity and friction
Status: Supporting source

Key contribution: provides mechanical basis for treating snap function as a coupled geometry/elasticity/friction problem rather than a clearance-only feature.
