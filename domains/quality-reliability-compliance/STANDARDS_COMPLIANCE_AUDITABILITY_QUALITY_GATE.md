## Compliance architecture
Requirement source -> applicability -> clause/requirement -> implementation/control -> objective evidence -> responsible owner -> revision/effectivity -> verification/audit -> change monitoring.

## Requirement-source families
- law/regulation
- product regulation/directive boundary
- management-system standard
- product standard
- material/process standard
- test/inspection standard
- customer requirement
- internal specification
- certification/approval condition

## Evidence objects
- approved specification/drawing
- certificate/material record
- process record
- calibration/MSA evidence
- inspection/test result
- qualification record
- training/authorization
- deviation/concession
- audit record
- corrective action
- release record

## Auditability principles
- evidence must be attributable
- revision/effectivity must be reconstructable
- records must be legible/retrievable/protected
- changes must preserve historical truth
- requirement-to-evidence links should be explicit for critical compliance claims

## Engineering principle
A certificate is evidence of a specific claim under a defined scope; it is not a universal substitute for verifying applicability, product conformity or process capability.
