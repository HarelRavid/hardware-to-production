## Inspection stages
### Incoming
Material/part identity, revision, supplier/lot, certificates, condition, dimensions/properties/tests as risk requires.

### In-process
Verify characteristics/process outputs at the operation where prevention/detection has highest value and before downstream cost obscures the cause.

### Final
Verify defined release requirements not already adequately controlled/evidenced upstream; final inspection is not a substitute for process control.

## Inspection-strategy objects
- 100% inspection
- sampling
- skip/reduced inspection boundary
- supplier evidence/certificate reliance
- automated inspection
- source inspection boundary
- first article/first-off
- patrol/process audit boundary

## Engineering principle
Inspection intensity should be driven by risk, process capability, measurement capability and evidence — not by an assumption that more inspection automatically creates more quality.
