# Section 6 — Quality, Reliability & Compliance — Domain Map

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Scope
The closed-loop quality system that converts requirements into controls, measurements, process evidence, nonconformance handling, reliability learning, traceability and compliance evidence across the product lifecycle.

## WBS
6.1 Quality planning
6.2 Incoming / process / final inspection
6.3 Measurement-system analysis (MSA)
6.4 Statistical process control and process capability
6.5 Nonconformance / MRB / CAPA
6.6 Reliability validation
6.7 Traceability and genealogy
6.8 Standards/regulatory overlays
6.9 Auditability and evidence retention

## Core loop
Requirement -> risk -> control -> measurement -> evidence -> decision -> nonconformance/learning -> corrective action -> updated requirement/control.

## Engineering principle
Quality is not an inspection department placed after manufacturing. It is an information-and-control system embedded in design, suppliers, processes, measurements, release decisions and feedback.

## Master decision object
### D-QRC-MASTER-001 — Is the evidence sufficient to release, continue, contain, correct or revalidate?
Inputs: requirement, risk, process state, measurement capability, inspection/test results, capability trends, nonconformance history, reliability evidence, traceability and applicable standards/regulation.

## Integrity rule
Compliance evidence, product conformity and process capability are related but distinct claims; one must not be substituted for another.
