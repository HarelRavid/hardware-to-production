## Engineering principles
A capability index calculated from an unstable process can be numerically precise and operationally misleading.

Control limits describe observed process behavior under the chosen model; specification limits describe product/engineering requirements. They are not interchangeable.

## Data integrity questions
- Are reworked/sorted units removed or retained transparently?
- Did process revision/material/tooling change inside the dataset?
- Are multiple cavities/machines/suppliers being incorrectly pooled?
- Is measurement variation material relative to process variation?
- Is the distribution/model appropriate?

No universal Cpk threshold is asserted across industries/customers.
