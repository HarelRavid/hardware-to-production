# 7.5 First Article / Sample Approval

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Scope
Controlled evidence that the supplier's defined process can produce conforming product against the released or specified requirement set before serial release.

## Approval objects
- sample/first article lot
- supplier/site
- drawing/spec revision
- material lot
- tooling revision
- process route
- special-process sources
- inspection plan
- dimensional report
- material/process certificates
- functional/test results
- cosmetic/visual standard boundary
- deviations
- approval status
- revalidation trigger

## Approval levels/boundaries
- first-off/sample
- first article inspection (FAI)
- PPAP-type production-part approval boundary
- customer-specific approval
- special-process qualification
- golden/boundary sample where appropriate

## Engineering principle
A conforming first article proves the inspected article met defined requirements; it does not by itself demonstrate stable serial process capability, capacity or long-term supplier control.

## Questions
1. Was the sample produced using production-intent tooling/process/material/operator conditions?
2. Were all drawing notes and non-dimensional requirements verified?
3. Are sub-tier special processes traceable and approved?
4. Were deviations hidden by handwork, sorting, rework or inspection selection?
5. What changes invalidate the approval?
6. Which characteristics require capability evidence beyond first-article conformity?

## Decision objects
### D-SCI-FAI-001 — Sample/FAI approval scope
### D-SCI-PPAP-001 — Is a production-part approval package required and at what depth?
### D-SCI-REVAL-001 — What supplier/process changes trigger re-approval?
### D-SCI-DEV-001 — Can an approval proceed with controlled deviations/open actions?

## Integrity rule
FAI/pass of samples must not be represented as proof of serial-process capability without additional capability/production evidence.
