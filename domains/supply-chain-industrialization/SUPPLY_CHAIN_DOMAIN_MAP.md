## Engineering principle
Supplier qualification is not a logo/checklist exercise. It is evidence that a specific supplier site and process can repeatedly make the required product state under controlled conditions at the required volume and quality.

## Master decision object
### D-SCI-MASTER-001 — Should this work be made internally, sourced externally, dual-sourced or transferred?
Inputs: core competence/IP, process criticality, capital, supplier capability, quality history, capacity, lead time, cost, flexibility, qualification burden, resilience, sub-tier risk, traceability and lifecycle/obsolescence.

## Integrity rule
A supplier's general reputation or certification does not prove capability for the specific part, process, material or production rate.
