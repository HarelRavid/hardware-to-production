## Engineering principle
A complete RFQ is an engineering-control artifact. If critical assumptions are left implicit, supplier quotes become incomparable because each supplier is pricing a different interpretation of the product/process requirement.

## Questions
1. Which requirements are truly mandatory versus design preference?
2. Are CTQs explicit enough for the supplier to design the process and inspection plan?
3. Are volumes/rates realistic and separated from future upside scenarios?
4. What supplier-developed process/tooling information must be reviewed before award?
5. Which evidence must exist before PO/tooling release versus before serial release?

## Integrity rule
Supplier confirmation that a requirement is 'achievable' is not equivalent to demonstrated process capability.
