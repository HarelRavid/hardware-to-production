# 7.1–7.2 Make/Buy & Supplier Selection

status: Researching
provenance: [GNR]

## Make/buy dimensions
- strategic/core competence
- IP/sensitivity
- process criticality
- CapEx/NRE
- internal capacity
- supplier ecosystem maturity
- quality/capability
- lead time/responsiveness
- cost structure
- volume/mix
- scalability
- regulatory/special-process requirements
- resilience/business continuity

## Supplier-selection dimensions
- specific process/equipment capability
- material experience
- dimensional/quality capability
- special-process controls
- metrology/test capability
- certifications/approvals as applicable
- engineering support
- capacity and expansion path
- lead time and logistics
- traceability/change control
- sub-tier management
- financial/business risk boundary

## Engineering principle
Lowest quoted piece price is not the same as lowest total acquisition or lifecycle cost. Supplier selection should include qualification, tooling, logistics, inspection, yield, rework, engineering support, inventory and disruption risk.

## Red flags
- supplier selected from machine list only
- quoted tolerance unsupported by capability evidence
- key special process entirely sub-tiered without visibility
- capacity statement unsupported by yield/utilization data
- no change-notification discipline
- prototype shop selected as scale supplier without industrialization plan

No universal supplier score weighting is asserted.
