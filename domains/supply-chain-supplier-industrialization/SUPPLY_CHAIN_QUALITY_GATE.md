## Integrity gates before Podcast Ready
1. Make/buy includes strategic, technical and lifecycle considerations.
2. Supplier certification is not treated as proof of part/process capability.
3. RFQ packages expose CTQs, processes, evidence and change-control expectations.
4. First article/sample approval is not treated as proof of serial capability.
5. Supplier/CM responsibility boundaries are explicit.
6. Alternate sources are technically qualified, not merely commercially approved.
7. Shared sub-tier dependencies are included in resilience analysis.
8. Supplier changes include process/site/material/sub-tier effects, not only drawing revision.
9. Obsolescence is managed with effectivity, risk and qualification logic.
10. GNR remains visible until verified.
