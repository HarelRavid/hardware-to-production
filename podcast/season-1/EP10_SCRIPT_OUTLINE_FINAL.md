## Misconceptions
- passing qualification proves field reliability;
- MTBF equals product lifetime;
- component qualification proves system qualification;
- repair PASS erases prior failure;
- one field return represents the whole population;
- every product should be field repairable.

## Closing action
Take one likely field failure and ask what unit/configuration/exposure/repair evidence would be needed to distinguish an anecdote from a population signal.
