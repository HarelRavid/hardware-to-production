Before:
“DFM means applying a checklist after CAD is done.”

After:
“I can connect product function, material, process, supplier, measurement and economics before the design locks in the wrong manufacturing assumptions.”

## Two-character opportunity
Rona asks:
“If the CNC prototype is perfect, why not just make the molded version from the same CAD?”
Oz shows that DFM is process-specific and claim-specific.

## Cold open
[ILLUSTRATIVE]
Machined enclosure works perfectly; molding later introduces draft, shrinkage, warpage, gating, inserts and tooling economics.

## Beat 1 — DFM is process matching, not a generic checklist
Claims: EP06-C01/C05.
Sources: S1C-S01/S02/S03.

## Beat 2 — Function and CTQ first
Claim: EP06-C02.
Start from functional need before selecting tolerance/process rule.

## Beat 3 — Material/process/equipment/supplier envelope
Claims: EP06-C01/C06.
Supplier DFM advice is evidence/input, not automatic authority.

## Beat 4 — Prototype-process transfer
Claim: EP06-C03.
Callback to A4/EP12.
What prototype proved vs what production route changes.

## Beat 5 — Measurement and inspection feasibility
Claim: EP06-C07.
Measurement system must be capable enough to verify the CTQ.

## Beat 6 — Cost per accepted good unit
Claim: EP06-C04.
Include yield/rework/inspection/tooling/cycle where material.
No universal threshold.

## Beat 7 — DFM Process-Design Contract
Function → stage → process → material/state → critical geometry → CTQ/tolerance rationale → process assumption → measurement → tooling/access → supplier/equipment → yield/rework → economics → evidence.

## Beat 8 — Widen the process window
Use architecture/geometry/interface choices before tightening controls reflexively.

## Beat 9 — Change control
Claim: EP06-C08.
Manufacturability change cannot silently alter product requirement.

## Misconceptions
- supplier preference is a design requirement;
- tight tolerance equals quality;
- prototype process success proves production capability;
- DFM belongs after design freeze;
- quoted piece price equals route economics.

## Closing action
Choose one critical part and write the intended production process/material/CTQ/measurement/economic assumptions next to the CAD feature.

## Handoff
EP06 makes the part manufacturable.
EP07 asks whether the whole product can be assembled correctly without hero operators.
