## 1. Decision

`Idea → Requirements → Ownership → Architecture/interfaces → Prototype strategy → Mechanical evidence → Electronics evidence → Verification intent → Configuration identity → Manufacturability gap → Build maturity → NPI ownership → Product vs Manufacturing Readiness → Lifecycle gates → DFM → DFA/mistake prevention → Test/calibration/traceability → Tolerance/variation → Reliability/service/field learning`

No frozen-backbone contradiction was found. No missing bridge requires restructuring the season before source/evidence verification.

## 2. Season promise achieved
The season now takes a listener from an idea or early prototype to a product-development system capable of making controlled decisions about production intent.

By the end of the season, the listener should be able to:
1. create a minimum useful requirement baseline;
2. map engineering responsibilities and ownership gaps;
3. define important system interfaces;
4. choose prototype methods based on the claim they need to test;
5. distinguish mechanical and electronics prototype evidence from cosmetic maturity;
6. plan verification before late-stage qualification;
7. reconstruct what exact configuration produced a result;
8. identify manufacturability evidence gaps;
9. describe build maturity without relying on labels;
10. map NPI/industrialization responsibilities;
11. separate product readiness from manufacturing readiness;
12. translate EVT/DVT/PVT/pilot/ramp names into evidence/decision gates;
13. perform a cross-process DFM review;
14. identify assembly ambiguity and mistake-proofing opportunities;
15. design test/calibration/traceability into the product-production system;
16. reason about functional tolerance and production variation;
17. connect reliability/service/repair and field failures back to controlled engineering evidence.
