## Audience target
Primary: founders, early technical teams and first engineering hires.
Secondary: NPI/industrialization engineers joining an immature product program.

Lifecycle emphasis:
Idea → Requirements → Team/ownership → Architecture/interfaces → Prototype technology choice → serious discipline prototypes → verification/configuration.

## Navigation behavior
Season 1 is not a mandatory linear course.

Entry points:
- A1 for idea/requirements-stage teams.
- A3 for teams already facing integration/interface problems.
- A4 for teams already prototyping.
- A5 for mechanical-development teams.
- A6 for electronics/embedded teams.

Each episode preserves standalone usability through short prerequisite recap rather than hidden dependencies.

## Wave-level listener promise
The Season 1 foundation now lets a small hardware team ask:
1. What are we actually trying to build and prove?
2. Which disciplines/owners are missing?
3. Which interfaces are likely to fail at integration?
4. Which prototype shortcuts are legitimate, and when do they expire?
5. What makes the next mechanical/electronics prototype more informative rather than merely prettier?

## Production gates
Each pack progresses through:
`RESEARCH PACK OPEN → CLAIM SET STABLE → EVIDENCE VERIFIED → TECHNICALLY REVIEWED → SCRIPT READY → PODCAST READY`

Claim-set stability is intentionally distinct from evidence verification. Standards-specific claims remain behind the Source Verification gate.

## Shared guardrails confirmed
- DEV/LVP/SVP are internal lifecycle lenses, not external standard labels.
- No assumption that every startup needs enterprise systems-engineering tooling.
- Component-level success does not prove system-level interaction.
- Prototype quantity does not equal readiness evidence.
- Prototype shortcuts require explicit expiration triggers where relevant.
- Cross-discipline interfaces require ownership and verification intent.
- Mechanical/electronics prototype maturity is claim/evidence based, not cosmetic.
- New external claims enter the Source Verification Backlog rather than being silently treated as established fact.
