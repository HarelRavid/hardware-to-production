## Primary sources

### EP13-S01 — NIST Solidification (2026)
https://www.nist.gov/publications/solidification-0

Use:
solidification, flow, segregation, porosity/inclusions and casting-process physics.

### EP13-S02 — American Foundry Society Casting Defect Analysis
https://www.afsinc.org/e-learning/casting-defect-analysis

Use:
gas-related vs shrinkage porosity/defect families.

### EP13-S03 — Forging Industry Association Product Design Guide
https://www.forging.org/Common/Uploaded%20files/Design%20Engineering%20Center/Product%20Design%20Guide%20for%20Forging.pdf

Use:
grain-flow, material/process/heat-treatment coupling and forging-design context.

### EP13-S04 — NIST Springback
https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/springback

Use:
springback as elastic shape recovery linked to material/process history.

### EP13-S05 — NIST NCAL tension-compression
https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/ncal-tension-compression-testing

Use:
advanced sheet material constitutive response and forming/springback context.

### EP13-S06 — ISO 230-2:2014 + Amd1:2016
https://www.iso.org/standard/55295.html

Use:
machine-axis positioning accuracy/repeatability context.

### EP13-S07 — ISO 230-12:2022
https://www.iso.org/standard/76756.html

Use:
finished-test-piece accuracy has multiple contributors; machine geometric errors are not the only source.

### EP13-S08 — NIST residual stress / machining study
https://www.nist.gov/publications/experimental-investigation-residual-stress-and-its-impact-machining-hybrid

Use:
mechanism example showing prior residual stress can create distortion during subsequent machining.

Boundary:
hybrid AM/subtractive study; no quantitative generalization to all metal routes.
