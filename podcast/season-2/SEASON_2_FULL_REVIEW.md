# Season 2 Full Review — Turn the Prototype into a Product

status: PASS WITH CONTROLLED EVIDENCE BACKLOG

## 1. Season mission result
PASS.

Season 2 successfully transforms the listener from “we have a working prototype” to an evidence-aware product-development posture capable of entering manufacturing-process selection and industrialization without treating prototype success, phase labels or one-good-unit results as production proof.

Canonical learning arc:
`Reality gap → Build evidence → NPI ownership → Readiness dimensions → Lifecycle gates → DFM → DFA → DFT/Traceability → Tolerance/Variation → Reliability/Service`.

## 2. Learning progression audit
PASS.

Episodes 1–5 answer what evidence is missing, how maturity should be described, who owns closure and how lifecycle decisions should be made.
Episodes 6–10 then modify the product-development mindset around manufacturability, assembly, testability, dimensional variation and lifetime/field behavior.

No episode requires hidden expert knowledge from a later episode to understand its core teaching. Episodes 6–10 benefit from the first half but remain independently enterable through recap.

2. EP03 must teach technical ownership, not sound like organizational consulting.
3. EP04 should visualize contrasting product/manufacturing readiness states.

6. EP09 should use one primary GD&T/GPS system per worked example.
7. EP10 should avoid turning into a full reliability-engineering statistics course; preserve architecture/decision focus.
