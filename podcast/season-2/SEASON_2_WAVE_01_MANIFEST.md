## Shared guardrails
- POC / prototype / MVP / production-intent are context labels, not universal standards-defined gates.
- Readiness is evidence-based and multidimensional; no single build label proves manufacturability.
- NPI is not a synonym for manufacturing engineering, program management or quality; responsibilities overlap but must remain explicit.
- Early startups may combine roles; responsibilities must not disappear.
- Production-intent does not mean final mass-production process/tooling is already frozen.
- A working prototype is valuable evidence and should not be framed as a failed production product.
