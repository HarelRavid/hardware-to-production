## 4. Audio/editorial rule

Season 2 must not become a manufacturing-process encyclopedia.

Every episode must prioritize:

mechanism
→ failure/variation
→ design tradeoff
→ production evidence
→ decision.

Dense process physics should be marked for companion visual/show notes where audio alone would become inefficient.

## 5. Shared process-family controls

Every process-family episode must state or preserve:
- exact material/state where relevant;
- process mechanism;
- dominant variation/defect mechanisms;
- geometry/process coupling;
- secondary operations;
- inspection/measurement evidence;
- supplier/equipment capability;
- qualification/applicability;
- DEV/LVP/SVP transfer;
- economics / cost per accepted good unit where relevant.

No generic numeric DFM folklore.

## 7. Workstream 06D — Joining / EP14

Required source families:
- welding process/qualification applicability;
- brazing/soldering applicability;
- adhesive bonding surface preparation/cure/process evidence;
- mechanical fastening/preload/inspection/serviceability;
- electronics soldering standards only where the final narration genuinely needs them.

Guardrails:
- no universal weld procedure/qualification standard;
- no universal adhesive strength/cure/preparation rule;
- no universal torque value or torque→preload equivalence;
- no IPC/J-STD requirement generalized beyond electronics applicability;
- no one “best joining process.”
