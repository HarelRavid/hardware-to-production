## 9. DEV / LVP / SVP discipline

The series does not treat production maturity as one universal calendar/waterfall.

Recurring distinction remains:
- DEV optimizes learning and uncertainty reduction;
- LVP creates repeatability/process/supplier/operator evidence with proportionate controls;
- SVP requires sustainable, capable, traceable, economically supportable production under defined conditions.

Shortcuts are not demonized; they are given explicit evidence boundaries and expiration triggers.

Result: **PASS.**
