## Listener promise
By the end, the listener can build an RFQ/first-production evidence package that forces supplier assumptions into the open and distinguishes sample conformity from production-process approval.

## Cold open
[ILLUSTRATIVE]
Three suppliers quote the same drawing. One assumes a different material condition, one subcontracts heat treatment, one inspects only a subset of characteristics. All three quotations look comparable—until the first parts arrive.

Question: Did the suppliers misunderstand the RFQ, or did the RFQ fail to define the same technical problem?

## Beat 1 — The RFQ is an engineering interface
Tags: [SYNTHESIS]
Claims: EP39-C01
Use: configuration, requirements, CTQs, material/process, inspection/test, documentation, packaging, change/deviation rules.
Guard: not every field applies to every part.

## Beat 2 — Configuration baseline and assumptions
Tags: [SYNTHESIS]
Wave 01 dependency.
Teach: drawing/model/BOM/spec revision; approved alternates; unclear assumptions become questions/deviations before approval.

## Beat 3 — First production approval asks what the evidence represented
Tags: [SYNTHESIS]
Claims: EP39-C02
Question: was it made with production-intent material, process, site, tooling, source and inspection method?

## Beat 4 — FAI
Tags: [AUTH-GUIDANCE]
Claims: EP39-C03
Source: W2-S12
Explain: IAQG 9102 standardizes first article inspection/documentation for aviation/space/defense product and can be adopted elsewhere.
Guard: do not imply universal applicability or AS9100 automatically requires 9102.

## Beat 5 — PPAP
Tags: [AUTH-GUIDANCE]
Claims: EP39-C03/C05
Source: W2-S05
Explain at high level: automotive production-part approval connects engineering requirements to actual production-run/process evidence.
Guard: no submission levels/elements/resubmission rules without licensed/current customer source.

## Beat 6 — FAI is not PPAP and neither is sustained capability
Tags: [SYNTHESIS]
Claims: EP39-C03/C04
Three questions:
- first article: does defined first-production/configuration output conform?
- production approval: is representative production/process evidence accepted?
- sustained capability: does process remain controlled/capable over time/rate/change?

## Beat 7 — Approval matrix
Tags: [SYNTHESIS]
Tool:
claim → evidence required → representative source/process/configuration? → result → deviation/open gap → owner → release decision → reapproval trigger.
Run a Sentinel molded enclosure supplier example.

## Beat 8 — Certificates and records
Tags: [SYNTHESIS]
Guard: certificates can be valid evidence objects when linked to actual requirement/source/lot; do not dismiss them categorically.

## DEV / LVP / SVP
DEV: supplier samples may answer design/process questions.
LVP: representative first-production evidence and explicit deviations become critical.
SVP: approval state must survive change, rate, site, tooling and supplier-network evolution.

## Misconceptions
- drawing-only RFQ is technically complete.
- one good sample = production approval.
- FAI and PPAP are the same.
- PPAP is universal.
- certificate = proof of everything.
- supplier approval never expires.

## Closing action
Pick one active RFQ and ask: what assumptions could two competent suppliers interpret differently? Add only the information needed to make those assumptions explicit.

## Handoff
EP40 asks what happens after approval, when suppliers, sites, materials, sub-tiers and demand start changing.
