Speaker A — Lead Host / Systems Engineer.
Speaker B — Practitioner / Challenger.

Season 4 challenge pattern:
- A introduces a quality/supplier evidence problem.
- B pushes against paperwork, cost or over-control.
- A distinguishes containment vs evidence vs authority.
- B asks what the team must actually know/record/ask the supplier.
- Both execute the listener tool.
- B summarizes the practical decision.

B must remain technically competent and should frequently challenge:
“Isn’t this just quality paperwork?”
“Why isn’t the certificate/sample/audit enough?”
“What exactly changes the decision?”
