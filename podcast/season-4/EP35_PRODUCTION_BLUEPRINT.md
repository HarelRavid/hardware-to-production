# Episode 35 Production Blueprint — Traceability, Genealogy and Evidence Retention

status: PRODUCTION BLUEPRINT COMPLETE
season: Season 4 — Quality, Suppliers and the Reality of Scale
lifecycle: LVP → SVP → FIELD

## Listener transformation
Before: equates traceability with serial numbers.
After: designs genealogy that links identity, configuration, materials, process, equipment, measurement, rework and release evidence so affected populations can be reconstructed.

## Narrative hook
Unit 1847 fails in the field. The serial number exists, but nobody can answer which supplier lot, firmware, calibration station, rework event or coating batch it saw. Identity without genealogy did not create usable traceability.

## Teaching flow
1. Identity versus genealogy versus record retention.
2. Unit, lot, batch and process-step relationships.
3. Configuration and effectivity linkage.
4. Supplier/material/sub-tier evidence.
5. Equipment, tooling, operator and recipe/version context where consequential.
6. Measurement, calibration, test, failure, rework and release history.
7. Granularity proportional to failure consequence and investigation need.
8. Retention, accessibility, integrity and change control.
9. Querying affected population after defect or supplier/process change.

## Core framework — Genealogy Evidence Chain
`Unit/lot identity → configuration/effectivity → material/supplier lot → process route → equipment/tool/recipe → measurement/test → deviation/rework → release → field/service history`.

## Listener tools
- Traceability Design Review.
- Affected-Population Query: what changed, when effective, which lots/units passed through, what evidence differentiates exposed from unexposed units?

## Common mistakes
- serial number = full traceability;
- storing data without version/context;
- final PASS erasing previous failure/rework;
- genealogy too coarse to isolate risk or so detailed it becomes unaffordable noise;
- retention periods copied without legal/customer applicability.

## Source/evidence backlog
Record-retention durations, regulated traceability, electronic-record controls and sector-specific genealogy requirements require exact legal/customer/standard applicability.

## Closing handoff
Episode 36 formalizes how teams determine which standards, regulations and customer requirements actually apply to the evidence they retain and the controls they operate.
