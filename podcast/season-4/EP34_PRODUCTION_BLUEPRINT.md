# Episode 34 Production Blueprint — Reliability Validation and Production Reality

status: PRODUCTION BLUEPRINT COMPLETE
season: Season 4 — Quality, Suppliers and the Reality of Scale
lifecycle: LVP → SVP → FIELD

## Listener transformation
Before: treats qualification or a few long-running units as proof of field reliability.
After: connects failure mechanisms, production variation, environmental exposure, configuration and field evidence into a controlled reliability argument.

## Narrative hook
Qualification passes. Six months later one supplier lot shows intermittent failures in a hot/humid market. The original test was not “wrong”; it simply did not prove every production/environment combination now being sold.

## Teaching flow
1. Reliability claim = mission/use + population + time/exposure + failure criteria.
2. Qualification versus ongoing production conformity.
3. Failure mechanisms and acceleration assumptions.
4. Production-process and supplier variation as reliability inputs.
5. Configuration/effectivity and environmental segmentation.
6. Field returns as structured evidence, not anecdotes.
7. Screening, burn-in and acceptance only where mechanism/economics justify them.
8. Design/process/supplier corrective loops after field signal.
9. Revalidation after consequential change.

## Core framework — Reliability Evidence Map
`Use profile → failure mechanism → design/process/supplier contributors → qualification evidence → production variation evidence → field exposure → failure signal → affected population → corrective action → revalidation`.

## Listener tools
- Reliability Claim Card.
- Field Signal Triage: configuration, lot, geography/environment, duty cycle, failure mode, repair history and comparator population.

## Common mistakes
- MTBF treated as lifetime;
- zero failures in a small sample treated as proof;
- accelerated test results transferred without model assumptions;
- component qualification treated as system qualification;
- field failures aggregated without configuration/exposure context.

## Source/evidence backlog
Any acceleration equation, sample-size/confidence claim, screening rule, environmental profile or reliability threshold requires exact source/model/applicability verification.

## Closing handoff
Episode 35 gives reliability and quality evidence the genealogy needed to identify the real affected population.
