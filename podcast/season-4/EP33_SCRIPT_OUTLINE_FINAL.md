## Cold open

[ILLUSTRATIVE]
Unit fails, gets reworked, retested and marked PASS. Three weeks later the same mechanism appears in another lot. The event was operationally closed but never converted into engineering learning.

## Beat 1 — Detect and identify the nonconformance
What failed?
Against which requirement/criterion?
Which product/process/configuration state?

## Beat 2 — Containment and affected population
Claim: EP33-C01.
Immediate question:
what else may share the same dependency?

## Beat 3 — Disposition is not corrective action
Claims: EP33-C02/C04.
Rework / repair / use-as-is / scrap / return.
Guard:
authority is application-specific.

## Beat 4 — Preserve failure history
Claim: EP33-C03.
Final PASS does not erase original FAIL/rework.

## Beat 5 — Root cause vs escape cause
Claim: EP33-C05.
Why did the defect occur?
Why did the system fail to prevent/detect it earlier?

## Beat 6 — Corrective action
Claim: EP33-C06.
Action must address evidenced mechanism/system boundary.

## Beat 7 — Effectiveness
Claim: EP33-C07.
Did future relevant populations actually change?

## Beat 8 — CAPA scope
Claim: EP33-C08.
Formal CAPA is sector/regulatory/system specific.
Do not require it for every defect.

## Listener tool — Nonconformance Learning Chain
Detection → containment → affected population → evidence preservation → disposition → cause → corrective action → change/effectivity → verification → effectiveness → systemic learning.

## Listener tool — Repeat-Failure Check
configuration / lot / supplier / operation / equipment / measurement / environment / rework / prior history.

## Misconceptions
- retest-to-pass closes the issue;
- containment is corrective action;
- CAPA for every defect;
- root cause can be chosen from intuition;
- action completion equals effectiveness;
- use-as-is is just a quality decision without requirement/risk authority.

## Closing action
Take one recently closed NCR and ask: what future population evidence proves the action worked?
