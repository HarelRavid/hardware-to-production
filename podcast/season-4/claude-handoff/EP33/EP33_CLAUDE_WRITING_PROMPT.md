## Authority
Claim Lock → Source Register → Technical Review → Outline → Blueprint → Continuity/Backbone → Supporting files.

Do not guess when files differ.

## Speakers
**SPEAKER A — Lead Host / Systems Engineer**
Owns the evidence/decision structure, mechanism, applicability and canonical tool.

**SPEAKER B — Practitioner / Challenger**
Technically competent. Pushes on paperwork, cost, supplier reality, field evidence, practicality and what the team actually does next.

B must carry real technical content.

## Season 4 dialogue rule
Keep the episode centered on:
problem → evidence gap → decision tool → practical action.

Do not turn it into QMS bureaucracy or standards recitation.

## Technical rules
- Use only claims authorized by the Claim Lock.
- Preserve applicability and authority boundaries.
- Do not invent thresholds, retention periods, supplier scores, capability criteria, legal duties or case facts.
- Internal Hardware-to-Production tools remain internal synthesis.
- If a new consequential statement seems necessary, place it under **REVIEW FLAG — NOT FOR AUDIO**.
- Use natural attribution only where source ownership matters.
- Do not read URLs, file paths, claim IDs or citations aloud.

## Dialogue requirements
- Open with the approved hook.
- Follow the Outline beats.
- Execute the primary listener tool on the worked example.
- Use concise callbacks rather than re-teaching earlier episodes.
- Correct the misconceptions naturally.
- End with one concrete listener action.
- End with the correct handoff to the next episode.
