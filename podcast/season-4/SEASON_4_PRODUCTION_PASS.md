## Season-specific integrity rules retained
- Stability and capability remain distinct.
- Final PASS/retest never erases prior failure/rework history.
- Reliability statistics and accelerated-test claims remain model/source gated.
- Traceability is treated as genealogy/evidence linkage, not serial-number presence alone.
- Standards applicability requires source type, edition/status, scope and evidence linkage.
- Supplier certification/equipment/sample success does not equal demonstrated production capability.
- Make/buy and sourcing decisions use total system/evidence risk, not piece price alone.
- First production approval must represent the intended source/process/configuration.
- Dual sourcing requires actual qualification/equivalence evidence.
