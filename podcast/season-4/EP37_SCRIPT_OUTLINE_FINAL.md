Rona:

Oz:
Because piece price is only one part of the capability/evidence/control decision.

## Cold open

[ILLUSTRATIVE]
A team outsources a process because the quote is cheaper. Every design change takes weeks, failure analysis is opaque and the supplier process cannot be independently assessed.

## Listener tool — Make/Buy Decision Model
capability/claim → strategic importance → internal maturity/capacity → supplier maturity → IP/learning → quality/evidence control → change speed → resilience risk → CAPEX/NRE → cost/good unit → decision → review trigger.

## Listener tool — Outsourcing Readiness Check
Do we understand the process well enough to specify, verify, change and recover it externally?

## Misconceptions
- cheapest quote wins;
- outsourcing transfers accountability;
- owning equipment means owning capability;
- utilization does not matter to internal CAPEX;
- IP/learning are “soft” non-economic issues;
- make/buy is a one-time decision.

## Closing action
Pick one outsourced or internal process and identify which capability/evidence you would lose if the current source disappeared tomorrow.

## Handoff
EP37 decides what to externalize.
EP38 asks whether the external source can actually perform the required process repeatedly.
