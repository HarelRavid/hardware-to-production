# Episode 40 Production Blueprint — Supplier Quality, Dual Sourcing and Resilience

status: PRODUCTION BLUEPRINT COMPLETE
season: Season 4 — Quality, Suppliers and the Reality of Scale
lifecycle: LVP → SVP → FIELD

## Listener transformation
Before: treats an approved supplier as a stable external vendor relationship.
After: manages the supplier as part of the production system, with quality signals, change control, recovery, alternate-source evidence and continuity risk.

## Narrative hook
A supplier performs well for a year, then changes a sub-tier process and the defect rate rises. At the same time, the backup supplier exists only on a spreadsheet and has never built the current revision. The company had two supplier names but only one qualified source.

## Teaching flow
1. Supplier quality after initial approval.
2. Incoming/receiving evidence proportional to source maturity and consequence.
3. Escapes, SCAR/CAPA and affected-population containment.
4. Supplier scorecards: quality, delivery, response, change and evidence — not one blended vanity score.
5. Supplier/process change notification and effectivity.
6. Sub-tier visibility and special-process dependence.
7. Dual sourcing: equivalence claims, tooling, material/process differences and requalification.
8. Capacity, geography, logistics and business-continuity risk.
9. Recovery plans and controlled source transfer.
10. Feed supplier lessons back into design, specifications and sourcing strategy.

## Core framework — Supplier Resilience Map
`Part/capability → current source → process/sub-tier dependencies → quality/capability evidence → change controls → capacity/logistics risk → alternate source → equivalence gaps → qualification evidence → recovery trigger`.

## Listener tools
- Supplier Quality Review Card.
- Dual-Source Readiness Check: current revision, tooling, material/process equivalence, measurement, capability, approved deviations, genealogy and capacity.

## Common mistakes
- approved once = approved forever;
- incoming inspection used as substitute for source control;
- dual-source count without actual qualification;
- supplier scorecard hides serious change/control weakness;
- unapproved sub-tier/process changes;
- contingency plan with no tooling/data/material/evidence transfer path.

## Source/evidence backlog
SCAR, supplier-change notification, dual-source qualification and resilience requirements depend on sector/customer/regulatory context. Exact flow-down and notification rules require authoritative applicability.

## Season closing
The listener now understands quality and compliance inside the production system and how to extend those controls across suppliers. Season 5 can therefore ask when and how to automate and connect a process without scaling instability or creating competing truth.
