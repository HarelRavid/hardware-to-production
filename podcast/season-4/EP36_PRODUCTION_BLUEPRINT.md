# Episode 36 Production Blueprint — Standards, Regulatory Requirements and Applicability

status: PRODUCTION BLUEPRINT COMPLETE
season: Season 4 — Quality, Suppliers and the Reality of Scale
lifecycle: LVP → SVP

## Listener transformation
Before: collects standards and requirements as a document library.
After: distinguishes regulation, standard, customer requirement, internal specification and guidance, and maps each applicable requirement to claims, evidence, scope, edition and owner.

## Narrative hook
A team proudly says it is “compliant with ISO.” Which ISO? Which edition? Which clauses apply to this product, process, market and supplier? Nobody can answer. The problem is not lack of documents; it is lack of applicability logic.

## Teaching flow
1. Regulation vs consensus standard vs customer requirement vs internal specification vs guidance.
2. Mandatory, contractual and voluntary pathways.
3. Product/process/market/site/supplier scope.
4. Edition/revision control and supersession.
5. Requirement extraction without inventing clause meaning.
6. Claim → requirement → evidence linkage.
7. Applicability statements and explicit non-applicability rationale.
8. Change monitoring and reassessment triggers.
9. Supplier flow-down and retained evidence.

## Core framework — Applicability & Evidence Register
`Source → type/authority → edition/status → scope → applicability statement → requirement/claim → evidence → owner → supplier flow-down → retention → change trigger`.

## Listener tools
- Applicability Decision Card.
- Standards Change Impact Check.

## Common mistakes
- “certified” interpreted as universal product compliance;
- using a standard outside its stated scope;
- mixing editions silently;
- citing paid normative text from secondary summaries;
- treating guidance as law or law as optional guidance;
- collecting PDFs without requirement-to-evidence linkage.

## Source/evidence backlog
All standards-specific teaching requires exact edition/status/scope verification. Regulatory claims require jurisdiction/date checks. Paid normative text must not be paraphrased beyond verified access.

## Closing handoff
The season now moves outside the factory walls. Episode 37 asks which capabilities should remain internal and which should be externalized deliberately.
