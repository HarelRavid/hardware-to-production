## Listener promise
Turn documents and search results into inspectable claim/evidence/applicability relationships without confusing retrieval with truth.

## Cold open
[ILLUSTRATIVE] A PDF proves a material test passed, but no one knows which requirement, product revision or later supplier change it applies to.

## Beat 1 — Document is container, not complete engineering truth
Claim EP49-C01.

## Beat 2 — Question → claim → evidence → source
Claim EP49-C02.
Reuse canonical publication contract.

## Beat 3 — Applicability/configuration envelope
Claim EP49-C03.
Evidence is not universally reusable.

## Beat 4 — Source says vs we conclude
Keep attribution and synthesis separate.

## Beat 5 — Change dependency
Claim EP49-C04.
Targeted reassessment, not blanket deletion.

## Beat 6 — Contradiction/uncertainty as first-class object
Claim EP49-C05.

## Beat 7 — AI synthesis maturity
Claim EP49-C06.
Model confidence/search rank ≠ evidence maturity.

## Listener tool — Claim Evidence Card
Question → claim → applicability → evidence → source/location → maturity/reviewer → dependency → decision use.

## Guardrails
Knowledge graph does not require graph database.
Protected standards content stays controlled/licensed.
No AI-generated conclusion silently promoted to verified fact.

## Closing action
Take one important PDF and write the exact claim it supports, the applicable configuration and what change would make you reassess it.

## Handoff
EP50 turns preserved knowledge into a decision workflow.
