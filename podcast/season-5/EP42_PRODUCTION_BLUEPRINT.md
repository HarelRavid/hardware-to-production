## Listener transformation
Before: evaluates automation mainly through operator savings and a simple payback period.
After: compares manual, semi-automated and automated routes using lifecycle economics, capacity, quality, risk and flexibility.

## Narrative hook
A cell promises a two-year payback from labor savings. Product volume arrives below forecast, engineering changes require expensive retooling and downtime exceeds the spreadsheet assumption. The calculation was arithmetically correct but economically incomplete.

## Teaching flow
1. Define the decision alternatives, including do-nothing and staged options.
2. Demand/rate scenario and product-life assumptions.
3. CAPEX, integration/NRE and commissioning.
4. Labor, consumables and utilities.
5. Yield/rework/quality effects.
6. Availability, changeover and maintenance burden.
7. Floor space, WIP and upstream/downstream constraint effects.
8. Design-change flexibility and residual/stranded asset risk.
9. NPV/payback/break-even only with explicit assumptions and sensitivity.
10. Staged CAPEX as an option when uncertainty is high.

## Core framework — Automation Business Case
`Need/constraint → alternatives → demand scenarios → CAPEX/NRE → operating cost → quality/yield → effective good throughput → maintenance/downtime → change flexibility → risk → sensitivity → staged/full/defer decision`.

## DEV / LVP / SVP
DEV: economics are directional; preserve option value. LVP: use observed process/rate data to compare assisted/semi-auto options. SVP: capital commitment should be supported by demonstrated demand, process stability, rate and maintainability.

## Listener tools
- Automation Business Case Sheet.
- Sensitivity Matrix for volume, uptime, labor, yield, product life and change cost.

## Common mistakes
- labor savings counted at 100% utilization;
- nominal machine rate used instead of accepted output;
- integration and maintenance omitted;
- quality improvement assumed rather than demonstrated;
- no downside volume scenario;
- sunk automation driving bad design/process decisions.

## Quantitative gate
All payback, NPV, capacity and break-even examples require explicit units, formulas, assumptions, sensitivity and independent arithmetic audit. Sentinel numbers must be labeled illustrative.

## Closing handoff
Episode 43 moves from whether to automate to how much: selecting assisted, semi-automated, robotic and vision functions at the right boundary.
