## Listener promise
Use context, alternatives, evidence and uncertainty to create reproducible manufacturing decisions instead of one-off search answers.

## Cold open
[ILLUSTRATIVE] Two engineers ask “Should we injection mold this?” One needs 50 units, the other 200,000. Same fact base; different decision context.

## Beat 1 — Retrieval vs decision
Claim EP50-C01.
Decisions need context and alternatives.

## Beat 2 — Atlas ownership
Claim EP50-C02.
ATLAS 10 is internal synthesis, not external standard.

## Beat 3 — Decision context
Lifecycle stage, volume, requirements/CTQs, product/process envelope, constraints.

## Beat 4 — Alternatives + evidence + uncertainty
Do not jump to one recommendation.

## Beat 5 — Hard stops vs weighted tradeoffs
Claim EP50-C04.
Safety/regulatory/evidence hard stop cannot be averaged away.

## Beat 6 — Record rationale/effectivity
Claim EP50-C03.
Preserve rejected alternatives/assumptions.

## Beat 7 — Outcome learning
Later production/field evidence feeds back to the decision.

## Beat 8 — AI role
Claim EP50-C05.
Assist navigation/synthesis; expose source/scope/uncertainty; human authority remains.

## Beat 9 — Atlas vs systems of record
Claim EP50-C06.
Link truth; do not create competing truth.

## Listener tool — ATLAS 10
Question → context → requirements → applicable knowledge → alternatives → evidence → trade-offs → decision → rationale/effectivity → outcome.

## Misconceptions
- best search result = best decision;
- one score can decide everything;
- Atlas should own all master data;
- AI recommendation = engineering approval;
- old decision remains valid after context change.

## Closing action
Replay one recent manufacturing decision: can another engineer reproduce why it was made?

## Handoff
EP51 addresses the semantic plumbing needed to connect the underlying systems.
