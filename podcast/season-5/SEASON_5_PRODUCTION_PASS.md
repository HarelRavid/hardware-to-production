## Season-specific integrity rules retained
- Automation is not a maturity badge; process/measurement/readiness evidence precedes commitment.
- Accepted sustainable throughput matters more than nominal machine speed.
- OEE remains a loss lens, not root-cause or system-capacity proof.
- Automated test is itself a controlled measurement/decision system.
- Parallel/replicated equipment is not assumed equivalent without evidence.
- Definition, execution/as-built state and evidence remain distinct but linked.
- System-of-record ownership is defined by information object/decision context rather than software branding.
- Rework and failed history are preserved in genealogy.
- Knowledge Graph claims retain source, applicability, configuration and maturity.
- Manufacturing Atlas supports decisions without becoming a competing PLM/QMS/MES.
- ISA-95/OPC UA are taught with scoped applicability rather than as mandatory architectures.
- OT/ICS security is integrated with manufacturing availability, safety, configuration and evidence integrity.
- Cyber/configuration recovery is not complete merely because machines are running again.
