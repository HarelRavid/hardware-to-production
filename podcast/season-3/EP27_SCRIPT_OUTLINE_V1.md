## Listener promise
Distinguish product verification from production-system validation and build an evidence matrix for the exact production envelope being evaluated.

## Cold open
[ILLUSTRATIVE] Product functional tests all pass, yet engineers manually fix stations, swap supplier parts and bypass test software during the build. Product evidence is strong; production-system evidence is weak.

## Listener tool — Production Validation Evidence Matrix
Claim → demonstrated envelope → evidence → intervention/deviation → result → gap → consequence → owner → decision.

## Misconceptions
- PVT label means production ready;
- product pass means factory pass;
- any engineer intervention invalidates everything;
- one short rate burst proves capacity;
- supplier samples prove source readiness.

## Closing action
For your next validation build, list every extraordinary intervention you expect. If the build needs it, define what claim becomes weaker because of it.
