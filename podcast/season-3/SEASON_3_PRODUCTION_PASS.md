## Season-specific controls
- LVP is taught before SVP-scale bureaucracy.
- Pilot is treated as an evidence-generating experiment, not a quantity milestone.
- Production validation is distinguished from product verification.
- Yield preserves first-fail/rework history rather than final PASS only.
- Capacity is based on accepted system throughput, not nominal machine speed.
- Supplier samples do not equal supplier readiness.
- Engineering change includes effectivity across product/process/supplier/tooling/test/WIP.
- Numerical thresholds and sector methods remain source/applicability gated.
