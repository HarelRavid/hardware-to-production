## Cold open

## Beat 1 — Document vs executable work

Claims: EP22-C01/C02.

Standard work may include:
prerequisites / sequence / critical parameters / tools / visual cues / acceptance / abnormal reaction / records.

## Beat 2 — Hidden expert knowledge

Claim: EP22-C03.

## Beat 3 — Job instruction and consistent teaching

Use NIST TWI as contextual support.

Guard:
do not prescribe TWI as universal requirement.

## Beat 4 — Training vs qualification

Claims: EP22-C04/C05.

Attendance/reading ≠ demonstrated competent execution.

No universal certification scheme.

## Beat 5 — Visual cues / defect exemplars / abnormal response

Claim: EP22-C02.

Teach what the operator needs to decide when the process leaves nominal state.

## Beat 6 — Revision/effectivity

Claim: EP22-C06.

Obsolete instructions at point of use are configuration failures.

## Beat 7 — Feedback from defects/rework

Claim: EP22-C07.

Recurring workaround should feed process/design definition.

## Beat 8 — Automation preview

Claim: EP22-C08.

Do not automate hidden ambiguity; understand the work first.

## Misconceptions

- WI = standard work;
- operator error ends the root-cause discussion;
- training attendance proves competence;
- more text means better instruction;
- experienced operators do not need controlled process definition.

## Closing action

Pick one operation and ask a new operator to identify the critical decision points without help from the expert builder.
