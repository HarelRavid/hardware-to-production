## Misconceptions
- perfect prototypes = production-ready source;
- supplier machine rate = supplier capacity;
- incoming inspection can compensate forever;
- CM owns everything because they build it;
- commercial PO terms automatically define technical change authority.

## Closing action
Pick one critical supplier and ask them to show which exact process/site/tooling/inspection route will make ramp units—not sample units.
