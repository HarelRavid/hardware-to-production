## 3. Season progression audit

Sequence:

`Failure reconstruction → success-pattern transfer → constrained startup decisions → automotive → medical → aerospace → long-life industrial equipment → full lifecycle synthesis`

### EP53 → EP54
Strong transition.
EP53 teaches how to reconstruct a failure without blame/hindsight. EP54 applies equivalent evidence discipline to success stories, where causal overclaim and survivorship bias are higher risks.

### EP54 → EP55
Strong transition.
After seeing large/mature industrialization patterns, the series deliberately asks what a resource-constrained hardware team can safely defer instead of implying mature-company practices should be copied early.

### EP55 → EP56
Strong contrast.
Startup proportional discipline is followed by automotive's highly connected quality/supplier system. The episode explicitly extracts mechanisms rather than presenting automotive as a maturity endpoint for all hardware.

### EP56 → EP57 → EP58
Strong industry-comparison arc.
Automotive emphasizes linked quality/supplier evidence; medical makes rework/remediation/field effectiveness and legal scope explicit; aerospace deepens configuration/approval/lifecycle assurance. Each industry teaches a different evidence problem rather than competing to be “best practice.”

### EP58 → EP59
Strong lifecycle extension.
Aerospace establishes long-lived manufacturing state/reliability evidence; industrial equipment shifts from product certification/airworthiness context to installed-base obsolescence, site retrofit and recommissioning.

### EP59 → EP60
Strong close.
External cases end before the final fictional composite. EP60 can therefore integrate the complete system without turning one real company's history into a universal lifecycle prescription.

Result: **PASS.**
