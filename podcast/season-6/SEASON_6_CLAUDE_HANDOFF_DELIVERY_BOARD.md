## 5. Causal-language rule

Every case claim must be tagged as one of:
- FACT;
- SOURCE INTERPRETATION;
- OUR FRAMEWORK LESSON.

Permitted causal verbs depend on source strength.

Never convert:
reported → proved;
associated → caused;
company claim → regulator finding;
charge/allegation → admitted liability.
