## SEASON 6 CASE RULE

Every case must be narrated in three layers:

1. **FACT** — what the source actually documents.
2. **SOURCE INTERPRETATION** — what the evidence supports at its own strength.
3. **OUR FRAMEWORK LESSON** — the Hardware-to-Production mechanism we transfer.

Do not blur the three.

## DIALOGUE REQUIREMENTS

1. Open with the approved hook in the Final Outline.
2. Make the listener transformation clear.
3. Use only the case facts needed to support the engineering lesson; do not narrate reports chronologically.
4. Preserve attribution naturally:
   - regulator says;
   - company reported;
   - investigator found;
   - source documents.
5. Execute the primary listener tool on at least one case/example.
6. State the transfer limit explicitly.

9. End with the correct conceptual handoff to the next episode.
