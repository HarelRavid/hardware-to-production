# 7. Causal-language risk audit

## Highest-risk cases
1. Note7 technical cause — company/independent-investigator attribution.
2. Tesla ramp — management/SEC description, not regulator causal finding.
3. Peloton — settlement distinguishes CPSC staff charges and Peloton non-admission.
4. Bolt — GM-attributed root cause in NHTSA-hosted manufacturer communication.
5. GE/GM/Ventec/Siemens/ABB — company/vendor success/project claims.
6. PW4077 numerical stress/life values — P&W technical estimates reported in NTSB factual material.

## Required verbs
Use only at source-supported strength:
- `determined` — where regulator/investigator/manufacturer explicitly says so and attribution is clear;
- `found` — source-specific finding;
- `reported/stated` — company/regulator attribution;
- `identified` — observed mechanism/feature;
- `contributed` — only when source uses/supports it;
- `associated` — when evidence is relational but not sole-causal;
- `illustrates/supports our framework lesson` — internal synthesis.

Avoid unsupported:
- `proved`;
- `the root cause was` without attribution/scope;
- `because` where the source only reports co-occurrence;
- `would have prevented` for tools/methods without counterfactual evidence.

Result: **PASS WITH SCRIPT-GATE CONTROLS.**
