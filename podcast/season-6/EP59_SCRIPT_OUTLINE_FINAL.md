## Listener transformation
Before: “Retrofit means replacing an obsolete component.”
After: “I can treat installed-base modernization as a controlled configuration transition across mechanics, controls, software, networking, safety, commissioning and recoverability.”

## Two-character tension
Speaker B:
“The new controller powers up and the machine runs. Why isn’t the retrofit finished?”

Speaker A:
because recovery/commissioning must restore the demonstrated production state, not only motion.

## Hook
The Siemens CCBSA modernization crossed PLCs, network, HMI, drives, diagnostics and backups—not one controller swap.

## Teaching flow
1. Installed-base identity includes more than hardware revision.
2. CCBSA — obsolescence across controls/network/HMI/support tools.
3. Backup ≠ recovery.
4. Safilin — retrofit crosses mechanics/drives/control/HMI/safety.
5. Change impact and recommissioning.
6. OSHA modification/lockout context — U.S. scope.
7. EU Machinery Regulation Article 18 — future/date-qualified substantial-modification context.
8. Installed-Base Change Review.
9. Practical action.

## Primary tool — Installed-Base Change Review
Installed configuration
→ obsolete/degraded element
→ interfaces affected
→ software/network/safety dependencies
→ WIP/product evidence impact
→ migration/rollback plan
→ commissioning/reverification
→ support/spares
→ restored trusted state.

## Guards
- Siemens benefits remain vendor/customer attributed;
- OSHA is U.S. workplace scope;
- EU general application is 20 Jan 2027, future at current date;
- no legal conclusion that a specific retrofit is “substantial modification” without analysis;
- no universal vendor architecture.

## Misconceptions
- component replacement is isolated;
- newer controller automatically improves production;
- backup file proves recoverability;
- successful power-up equals recommissioned production;
- legal obligations are the same across jurisdictions.

## Closing action
Pick one legacy machine and draw every interface/dependency that changes if its controller, drive or safety subsystem is replaced.
