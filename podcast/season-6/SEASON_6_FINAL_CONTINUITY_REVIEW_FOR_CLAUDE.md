## 4. Source-language continuity

Every real case follows:

**FACT → SOURCE INTERPRETATION → OUR FRAMEWORK LESSON**

Claude must not blur the three layers.

### FACT
Use source-owned language and exact population/date/configuration where material.

### SOURCE INTERPRETATION
Explain what the source supports without expanding causality.

### OUR FRAMEWORK LESSON
Explicitly label the transferable engineering mechanism as Hardware-to-Production synthesis.

Season-wide prohibited transformations:
- company statement → regulator finding;
- allegation/charge → admitted fact;
- recall population → confirmed defective population;
- potentially involved → defective;
- vendor case → independent proof;
- correlation/association → sole causation;
- successful outcome → proof the practice caused success;
- standards context → universal obligation.
