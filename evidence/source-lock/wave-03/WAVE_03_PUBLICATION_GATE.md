### W3-P02 — Product pass and production-system pass are different

### W3-P03 — Final output can hide rescue
Rework history is required to understand process maturity, capacity and cost.

### W3-P04 — Capacity is a system claim
Takt, cycle time, accepted throughput, WIP, downtime, yield and rework must remain distinct.

### W3-P05 — Supplier ramp readiness is current-source evidence
Prototype sample approval does not automatically establish source capacity/capability at ramp.

### W3-P06 — Change release is not implementation
Effectivity, WIP/inventory disposition, supplier cut-in and post-change effectiveness close the transition.

### W3-P07 — Economics is downstream of production evidence
NPV/payback/sensitivity models should consume credible demand/yield/capacity/maintenance assumptions rather than overwrite weak evidence with financial precision.
