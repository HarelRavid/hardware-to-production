## Cross-process rule

Each sub-wave must separately control:
- material state;
- process mechanism;
- defect/variation mechanisms;
- geometry/process constraints;
- secondary operations;
- measurement/inspection;
- supplier/equipment capability;
- qualification/applicability;
- DEV/LVP/SVP transfer;
- economics/cost per accepted final part.

No numeric process-design rule is transferable across materials/equipment/suppliers merely because it is common online guidance.
