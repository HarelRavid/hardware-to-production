## 2. Mechanical fasteners

Result: PASS.

Core lesson:
torque, friction and clamp force are related but not interchangeable as universal values.

Required guards:
- no generic torque/preload formula or torque table;
- no universal locking method;
- no assumption serviceable = lower integrity;
- fastener property class alone does not prove assembled joint performance.

## 3. Fusion welding

Result: PASS.

Core lesson:
welding introduces process/metallurgical/thermal/distortion variables and evidence layers.

Required guards:
- no universal weld procedure qualification scheme;
- ISO 3834 / ISO 15614 / ISO 9606 are examples of scoped welding-quality/procedure/personnel frameworks, not universal mandates;
- no weld-acceptance/NDT/preheat/heat-input numbers;
- avoid saying a qualified welder or qualified procedure alone proves every production joint.

Revision watch:
- ISO 15614-1 replacement advanced.
- ISO 9606-1 replacement project active.

## 4. Adhesive bonding

Result: PASS.

Strong source support:
- surface preparation = process input;
- fabrication/reporting records matter;
- coupon tests have transfer limits.

Required guards:
- no universal surface preparation;
- no universal bondline/cure/strength;
- no claim final visual inspection proves bond quality;
- durability remains material/joint/environment specific.

Revision watch:
ISO 21368:2022 current, replacement project underway.

## 5. Brazing / soldering

Result: PASS.

Required guards:
- do not merge structural brazing and electronics soldering into one qualification regime;
- J-STD-001J only in electronics context;
- ISO 13585 is brazing-personnel qualification context;
- no filler/temperature/clearance numeric guidance.

Revision watch:
ISO 13585:2021 expected to be replaced soon.

## 6. Hybrid joints

Result: PASS as synthesis.

Guard:
Do not imply two joining methods automatically improve reliability.
A hybrid can add load paths/sealing/redundancy but also adds interfaces, compatibility issues and process controls.

## 7. Inspection / evidence

Result: PASS.

Teach:
- inspectability is process/function specific;
- coupon qualification, process qualification, operator/equipment state and real-geometry evidence answer different questions;
- destructive/NDT selection is application-specific.

No universal NDT method or acceptance criteria.
