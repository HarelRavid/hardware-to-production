## Locked publication findings

1. metal route defines material/process state as well as geometry;
2. casting requires solidification/defect reasoning;
3. porosity is mechanism/location/function dependent;
4. forging/forming can alter directional material structure;
5. sheet forming can exhibit material/history-dependent springback;
6. machine-axis accuracy is not finished-part process capability;
7. residual stress/process history can move parts during later machining;
8. exact alloy/state/heat-treatment belongs in route definition;
9. near-net + finish machining is a legitimate hybrid route;
10. inspection/NDT burden belongs in process-route comparison;
11. no universal metal-process numeric rule belongs in EP13.
