# ADR-003 — Content Provenance and GNR Traceability

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Any content initially generated by an AI system must carry provenance marker `GNR` (Generated), even after later human review or source verification.

`GNR` records origin, not truth status. Verification is recorded separately.

## Rules

1. AI-originated Objects, Claims, Decisions, Relationships, summaries, tables and recommendations must include `provenance: [GNR]` or `GNR` in their provenance field.
2. `GNR` is never removed solely because the content was verified later.
3. Verified evidence tags may be added alongside it, for example: `provenance: [GNR, STD, PPR, MFG]`.
4. A Claim whose only provenance/evidence is `GNR` cannot be used as a Podcast Ready engineering assertion.
5. GNR-generated numerical guidance must remain clearly separated from externally sourced values until independently supported.
6. Project synthesis produced by AI may be marked `GNR + SYN`; source-backed synthesis must also reference the underlying source IDs.
7. Human-authored content may use `HUM`; expert interview-derived content may use `EXP`.

## Controlled Provenance Codes

- `GNR` — AI generated
- `STD` — Standard / normative document
- `GOV` — Government / public research institute
- `PPR` — Peer-reviewed research
- `BK` — Book / professional handbook
- `UNI` — University course / institutional academic material
- `MFG` — Manufacturer technical literature
- `IND` — Industry guidance / association / industrial best practice
- `EXP` — Human expert input
- `HUM` — Human-authored project content
- `SYN` — Project synthesis combining evidence

## Consequence

Provenance and verification are independent dimensions. A record can therefore be both `GNR` and `Verified` when its origin is AI-generated but its engineering content has been checked against acceptable sources.
