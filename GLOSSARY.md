# Glossary

This glossary will standardize terminology used across industries and prevent stage names from being treated as universal definitions.

## Initial terms

### POC — Proof of Concept
Evidence that a technical principle can work. A POC does not demonstrate production readiness.

### MVP — Minimum Viable Product
The smallest product configuration capable of validating defined user or market assumptions. In hardware, an MVP may still rely on non-production parts and processes.

### EVT — Engineering Validation Test
A stage focused on proving that the engineering design and architecture meet intended functional requirements.

### DVT — Design Validation Test
A stage focused on validating a near-final design against product requirements, environmental conditions, reliability expectations, and compliance needs.

### PVT — Production Validation Test
A stage focused on validating the production process, tooling, test equipment, documentation, people, suppliers, and controls using production-intent conditions.

### NPI — New Product Introduction
The cross-functional process used to prepare, validate, launch, and stabilize a new product in production.

### Industrialization
The work required to convert a product design into a capable, controlled, economical, and scalable manufacturing system.

### Design Transfer
The controlled transfer of product and process knowledge from development into manufacturing and operations.

### DFM — Design for Manufacturing
Design practices that improve manufacturability, process capability, cost, yield, and scalability.

### DFA — Design for Assembly
Design practices that simplify assembly, reduce part count, prevent errors, and improve repeatability.

### DFT — Design for Test
Design practices that enable adequate, efficient, reliable, and traceable verification during production and service.

### EBOM — Engineering Bill of Materials
The product structure as defined by engineering intent.

### MBOM — Manufacturing Bill of Materials
The product structure organized for procurement, manufacturing, assembly, packaging, and production execution.

### Yield
The proportion of units or operations that meet requirements without unacceptable defect.

### First-Pass Yield
The proportion that passes a process or test without rework, repair, retest, or repetition.

### Ramp-up
The controlled increase in production volume while capability, yield, cost, quality, staffing, and supply are stabilized.

### CTQ — Critical to Quality
A measurable characteristic that has significant impact on customer needs, safety, function, compliance, or downstream process performance.

### Golden Unit
A controlled reference unit used to verify test systems, compare performance, or support troubleshooting. Its intended use and limitations must be defined.
